## Summary

**Null result, with two exact obstructions that explain it.** No reading of "scale map" that the corpus supports makes the staggered octave operator dynamically self-similar. Under every coarse-graining tested (three octave-to-station maps and a 2:1 harmonic map), on the open chain, the sequential ring and the beat-synchronous ring, for F and for T = κ ∘ F at the measured α, the defect stays between 0.88 and 1.00 on a scale where 0 is exact and 1 is uncorrelated, at every m from 1 to 8, with no dip out to m = 49 and no improvement with depth. At m = 4 and m = 8 the corpus maps are indistinguishable from random maps of the same shape. The pre-stated rule (defect ≤ 0.10 and below the 1st percentile of every null) is met in 0 of 48 cells. The only cells that beat the random nulls (the whole-mode map at m ≤ 3, still at 0.88 to 0.92) are explained by a frozen-image effect, not by self-similarity.

The condition holds exactly in one place: when the "next scale" is read as the next octave along the chain (R = the octave shift), at m = 1. That is translation invariance, which is precisely the "same rule at every level" the review set aside; at every m ≥ 2 it fails, with a closed form. Two theorems say why coarse-graining fails: (1) no translation-covariant octave-to-station projection can push the defect below about 0.50, for any m, because the coarse octave is not homogeneous at the station level while the fine chain is homogeneous at the octave level; (2) the fixed point cannot map to the fixed point under any coarse-lattice-covariant map, because coarse-graining sends the attractor's momentum q to 7q, and q* ≈ 0.362π is not a fixed point of that map (the fixed points are the multiples of π/3).

## 1. The test

For a fine system (n_f octaves) and a coarse one (n_c octaves, same construction), the defect of a linear map R is

  δ(m) = min over φ of ‖R A^m − e^{iφ} B R‖_F / √(‖R A^m‖² + ‖B R‖²),

with A, B the fine and coarse F (or T). δ = 0 means exact up to a global phase (which a unitary cannot observe); δ = 1 means the two compositions are uncorrelated. The Frobenius norm is the RMS over random input states, so δ is a state-averaged defect. The primary depth is n_c = 2 (fine 14 octaves, 98 to 99 nodes); depth trends use n_c = 1, 2, 3.

## 2. The scale maps, and what the corpus supports

The corpus carries two pictures of "the next scale", and the test needs both because they give opposite answers.

**Adjacency: the staggered octave's own geometry.** §27.7t.4 lays successive scales end to end on one continuation axis: octave n occupies [3.5n, 3.5(n+1)] and meets octave n+1 only at the shared tonic. "The individual of octave n + 1 is the whole produced by octave n" (`docs/the_staggered_octave.html` §3). The 2026-07-30 harmonics passage names this direction "scales reached one after another through time (that is ⟳, the tonic, 3.5D = 0D′)". Read this way, the scale map is the octave shift, a same-size map: **R_shift**. On the open chain, **R_peel** collapses the bottom octave into its closure tonic.

**Coarse-graining: a completed octave as one station.** The same corpus reads a completed whole, seen from the next scale, as a single point. "From scale Λ (top-down view), ⊙λ appears as •" (CLAUDE.md, the four readings); "Every point, heard from outside, is a completed boundary" (framework §4.9); "an individual IS a •" (`docs/the_staggered_octave.html` §3). Read this way, R sends each completed fine octave to one coarse station. One fine octave is R = 7 half-steps and takes the place of one coarse half-step, so seven fine octaves make one coarse octave. Two realizations:

- **R_dec** (tonic decimation): coarse node j is fine tonic 7j, the shared node where fine octave j−1 closes and fine octave j opens. This is 3.5D = 0D′ taken literally: the closure node IS the station.
- **R_whole** (speaker shift): coarse node j is the completed fine octave j−1 projected onto its own whole, the ℂ⁸ attractor of T (structural share 0.687, as in v10). **R_unif** uses the same window with uniform weights, as a control on the weighting.

**Harmonic refinement: simultaneous nesting.** The 2026-07-30 string model: finer scales are more nodes within one fixed extent, an octave up being f₂ = 2f₁. Natural ratio 2, natural m = 2: **R_harm**. On the staggered lattice a 2:1 decimation acts on residues as multiplication by 2 on ℤ₇ (0→0, 1→2, 2→4, 3→6, 4→1, 5→3, 6→5), which sends processual stations to structural ones and back, so the lattice does not carry this reading natively. The corpus itself assigns the harmonic model to a different direction than the tonic.

**Which the corpus supports.** Adjacency and coarse-graining are both explicit. Coarse-graining is what the review and the brief mean by a scale map: many fine degrees of freedom onto fewer coarse ones, with a time dilation m. Adjacency is the staggered chain's literal geometry, and it is exactly the "same rule at every level." The harmonic reading belongs to the simultaneous-nesting direction and is not native to this lattice; it is tested for completeness.

**What m means here.** One application of F is one complete four-stroke cycle (i¹, i², i³, i⁰). Four coarse strokes make one coarse F, so m = 4 is "one completed fine cycle per coarse stroke", the stroke-level form of "a completed octave takes the role of one station"; m = 8 is two fine cycles per coarse stroke. E7 also tests the stroke-resolved and literal stroke-level versions directly.

**F2 (grade A, exact): the symmetry floor.** On the beat-synchronous ring the fine F commutes with the octave shift U_f exactly (v16). If R is translation-covariant (R U_f = S R, S the coarse one-node shift), then for every m and every phase c,

  (R F_f^m − c F_c R) − S (R F_f^m − c F_c R) U_f† = c (S F_c S† − F_c) R,

so δ ≥ ‖(S F_c S† − F_c) R‖_F / (2 √(‖R F_f^m‖² + ‖F_c R‖²)). For a co-isometric R the floor is R-independent: δ ≥ ‖S F_c S† − F_c‖_F / (2√(2 N_c)) = 0.4996, 0.5004, 0.4976, 0.4975 at n_c = 1, 2, 3, 4 (T agrees within 0.0003). Verified on 36 translation-covariant maps (decimations at every residue, the whole-mode and uniform windows, 20 random-weight windows, the optimized windows) across m = 1..8: the smallest margin above the floor is 0.363, never negative. The m-independence is the point: no time dilation can rescue any translation-covariant octave-to-station map. The reason is structural: the fine chain is homogeneous at the octave level (every octave identical), so its image is homogeneous at the coarse-node level, while the coarse octave's seven stations play different roles.

**F3 (grade A, exact): the attractor selection rule.** Any R covariant at the coarse-lattice level (seven fine octaves shift to one coarse octave; this covers every ratio-7 map tested here, including the station-dependent family) sends a fine Bloch mode of momentum q per octave to coarse momentum 7q. For the 2:1 harmonic map the same argument gives q → 2q, whose only fixed point is q = 0, so it is excluded too. Fine and coarse rings are the same construction, so both attractors sit at q*, and ⟨ψ_c, R ψ_f⟩ vanishes unless 7q* ≡ q* (mod 2π), that is, unless q* is a multiple of π/3. At the measured α, q* = 0.362110π (recomputed; v16: 0.3621π), offset +0.0288π (+5.18°) from π/3, and its image 7q* = 0.5348π. The α → 0 limit q*₀ = 0.362487638892π (v17) is offset +0.0292π (+5.25°), image 0.5374π. Both land in a different momentum sector, so on the infinite ring the overlap is exactly zero. Finite rings confirm it (overlap ~1e-14 at n_c = 2 and 3 for all three maps). At n_c = 4 the discretized momenta happen to coincide (7q_f = Q_c = π/2), and the overlap there (0.415) is at the within-sector chance level (0.357 ± 0.165). On the open chain, which has no momentum, the tonic-decimation overlap (0.24, 0.19, 0.007 at n_c = 1, 2, 3) is at or below the random-vector level (0.32, 0.24, 0.19). The weight-level version fails for the same reason: the fine attractor's per-octave weights are exactly uniform (max/min = 1.000000000, as v16 found), so any block-summed image is uniform across coarse nodes, while the coarse attractor's station shares within an octave are 0.064, 0.015, 0.414, 0.176, 0.019, 0.034, 0.279.

**F4 (grade A, exact): the adjacency reading holds at m = 1 and only there.** With R the octave shift on the sync ring, R F = F R exactly (v16: commutator 3.5e-15; here δ(1) = 0.000), and for m ≥ 2 the defect is the closed form δ(m) = √(1 − |tr F^{m−1}| / N), matched by the numerics to all printed digits: 0.000, 0.857, 0.904, 0.917, 0.960, 0.960, 0.979, 0.725 for m = 1..8. On the open chain, peeling the bottom octave into its closure tonic gives 0.290 at m = 1 (the residue is the bottom seam) and 0.82 to 0.98 for m ≥ 2. Under the shift the attractor also maps to itself up to the phase e^{iq*}, because it is a Bloch mode (v16): exact again, and again the same-rule property. This is the review's distinction made exact: translation invariance in scale, the same rule at every octave, is satisfied; dynamical self-similarity with any time dilation is not.

**F5 (grade B+): the only cells that beat the nulls are a frozen-image effect, not self-similarity.** R_whole beats every null at m = 1 to 3 (5 of the 48 cells), with defects 0.88 to 0.92, far from small. Mechanism (E3b): the whole-mode coincides with one eigenvector of the single-octave F₈ (overlap 1.0000; the other seven at most 0.008), so R_whole reads each fine octave in a near-eigenmode of its own beat block and its coarse image barely moves (frozen defect δ(R F^m, R) = 0.54 to 0.55 at m = 1, rising with m). A frozen image can overlap F_c R only through the identity (trace) part of F_c, whose level is δ(R, F_c R) = 0.857 on the ring and 0.843 on the chain (√(1 − |tr F_c|/N_c) = 0.859 and 0.848). R_whole sits just above that level, and from m = 4 on it is no longer distinguishable from the nulls (worst-case percentile 9% to 98%) as leakage through the shared tonics unfreezes the image. The same mechanism appears in F7 below: letting the coarse beat angle renormalize, the best fit runs to θ′ → 0, the frozen coarse step.

**F6 (grade B): corpus-shaped family ceilings are set by geometry and fitting capacity, not by the octave.** Optimizing R over whole families (exact in the weights by a generalized Rayleigh quotient, global phase by scan): translation-covariant octave windows (8 weights) reach 0.86 to 0.88 (above the F2 floor, as they must); station-dependent windows (weights depending on the coarse residue, 56 weights) reach 0.62 to 0.64; two-octave station-dependent windows (105 weights) reach 0.47 to 0.49. None is small. The octave does no work in these fits: windows misaligned with the octave (s = 1..6) fit as well (translation-covariant family, within 0.005) or better (station-dependent family: the aligned window is worse than the best misaligned one at every m, by 0.011 to 0.056). The canonical four-beat octave does no work either: on the sequential ring its station-dependent ceiling (0.641 at m = 4, 0.643 at m = 8) sits at the 20th and 15th percentile of 40 random-octave-block constructions with identical tonic-shared geometry (0.686 ± 0.046 and 0.701 ± 0.045), and swapping in a random fine block under the canonical coarse operator changes nothing (0.642 ± 0.011). Only non-local dynamics does worse (Haar-random fine unitary: 0.849 ± 0.004). The ceilings are depth-independent (n_c = 1, 2, 3).

**F7 (grade A for the closed form, B for the comparison): the tensor-nesting null.** Tensor nesting (v11 / v14-C512 κ, depth 2 and 3, R = contraction of the finest factor with the C8 whole-mode) is exact at m = 1 (0.000 with an F₈ eigenvector as the contraction vector, 0.004 with the whole-mode, 0.009 for T, the residue being the O(α) cross-scale bonds): separable nesting satisfies R ∘ F = F ∘ R trivially, the same rule at every level. For m ≥ 2 the pure-F defect is exactly δ² = 1 − |tr F₈^{m−1} / 8|^{depth−1}, which compounds toward 1 with depth (m = 4: 0.847, 0.959, 0.989 at depth 2, 3, 4; m = 8: 0.883, 0.975, 0.995). This parallels the departure, which compounds under tensor nesting (0.64, 1.69, 2.74 α at depth 1, 2, 3) and stays in the v14 band for the staggered chain (0.61 to 0.78 α up to 14 octaves). The staggered defects do not compound with depth either (F1, F6), but they saturate near 1, not near 0. Comparison: neither construction is self-similar at any m ≥ 2. The staggered chain differs from the tensor one in not compounding, which is a property of locality, not of self-similarity.

**F8 (null, robust): the remaining readings and knobs.** The stroke-level readings fail: one completed fine cycle per coarse stroke, checked stroke by stroke (R F_f^k against the first k coarse strokes, k = 1..4), gives 0.96 to 0.99 for R_dec and 0.87 to 0.97 for R_whole, against a Haar mean of 0.95 to 0.99; the literal "m fine strokes per coarse stroke" gives 0.86 to 0.98. Letting the coarse beat angle renormalize does not rescue the corpus maps (best R_dec 0.942 at θ′ = 0.85π, against 0.981 at the canonical π/2), and the best station-dependent fit is reached at the frozen end, θ′ ≈ 0.03π (0.276 at m = 4, 0.271 at m = 8), not at π/2 (0.632, 0.640): the canonical angle is not a fixed point of octave-to-station coarse-graining. Renormalizing α in the coarse T moves the defect by less than 0.002 between α′ = 0 and α′ = 10α.

## 4. What this says about the framework (readings, flagged)

*Reading, grade C.* The staggered octave operator is scale-homogeneous: one rule repeated at every octave, exactly so on the synchronous ring (F4). That is the content of the corpus's existing operator-level A3 statements: v14-C512's "A3 outer/inner identical" and v16's "every octave carries identical weight; scale self-similarity holds to machine precision on the ring". This experiment shows that homogeneity is not dynamical self-similarity under a scale map, so by the review's standard v16's phrase describes scale homogeneity (a wording note for whoever next touches v16 or §27.7t.7; nothing is rewritten here). Bearing on CLAUDE.md open decision 2 (the self-similarity axiom's home), as input and not a decision: the candidate theorem "the circuit exits ⟳ and re-enters • unchanged in form, so every scale runs the same engine" states homogeneity, which holds; read as R ∘ F^m = F ∘ R it would not hold for the current operator.

*Reading, grade C: what self-similarity would require.* F2 and F3 turn the null into a specification. (i) The fine octaves must carry, at the octave level, the differentiation the coarse octave has at the station level: a chain whose seams are modulated by the coarse station pattern, recursively (a substitution-type chain with inflation factor 7), rather than a homogeneous one. (ii) The attractor's phase advance per octave must be a fixed point of q → 7q, a multiple of π/3 (a phase whose return period divides six octaves); the canonical q* misses the nearest one by about 5.2°. (iii) The beat angle must be a fixed point of the coarse-graining flow; here the best fits run toward θ′ → 0. None of this is built or claimed.

*Reading, grade C: the review's second suggestion is already the operator's structure.* Separating the cycling phase from the developing whole is how the attractor is built (v16): per-octave weights return exactly (chroma) while the phase advances q* per octave (height). F3 locates the obstruction in the developing part: the advance per octave is not invariant under the scale map.

*Unaffected.* The Shared-Tonic Principle (v14 F1; departure saturates rather than compounds) is a conservation statement and stands; this experiment adds that the self-similarity defect does not compound either, while staying large. v15's composition-order result and the v16 to v19 band results are untouched.

## 5. Scope and open items

- R acts on amplitudes. The attractor is also checked at weight level (F3). A weight-level dynamical test (block-summed |ψ|² under F^m) is weaker and is not run here.
- Maps that are not covariant across the coarse lattice (weights that change from one coarse octave to the next) can fit anything given enough freedom, so they are not scale maps for a homogeneous chain. They are excluded by design.
- The corrected-beats F (queued in the corpus) is not tested; every number here is a property of the legacy-coordinate realization, like v14 to v19.
- Next instrument, if pursued: a hierarchically seam-modulated chain per (i) above, tested with the same harness and nulls.
