## What Is That?


My mother said I should write the book.

This is it: the list of what I found when the pointing finally reached the biggest things. What follows is me saying what these things are.

---

## How to Read This Book

Every war begins in a dictionary.

Before people fight over anything, they have already accepted definitions: of what they are, of what others are, of what truth is, of what counts. Most of those definitions were never chosen. They were inherited, absorbed from a culture that absorbed them from another, and some of them are wrong in ways that quietly run your life.

This book does one thing. It takes the biggest words we have, the ones underneath all the other words, and defines them again, carefully, so that they fit together. Not new words; old words, given back their referents. Each entry names the definition you probably inherited, offers the replacement, and says in a few sentences why the replacement earns its place.

The definitions form one picture. Read in order, they assemble it. Read alone, each one still stands.

One rule governs every entry: the bold sentence is the definition, and everything after it explains, tests, or connects; nothing after the bold sentence is allowed to smuggle a further claim into the definition itself.

Start with the word under all the others.

## I. The Ground

### Reality

*Instead of:* the physical universe; the collection of all objects.

**Reality is what is.** One, inexhaustible, prior to every naming. Not the sum of things, because a sum is a description and Reality precedes its descriptions. Everything actual participates in it; no description exhausts it; and it does not need to be believed to continue being what it is.

### Truth

*Instead of:* a property of statements; whatever survives fact-checking; whatever my side says.

**Truth is Reality.** Not your statement, not my statement, not any institution's statement. Statements can be *true*; Truth is what they are true *of*. This one move disarms both cynics and fanatics: the relativist notices that claims are fragile and wrongly concludes Truth must be; the dogmatist notices that Truth is non-negotiable and wrongly concludes his claim must be. Both confuse Truth with a claim about it. Truth cannot become false. Our claims can.

### true

*Instead of:* verified; agreed upon; officially accepted.

**To be true is to correspond with Truth.** Correspondence relates a claim to Reality; agreement relates people to each other. A claim can be true while everyone denies it, and false while everyone applauds. The lonely accurate witness and the confident deluded crowd are both real, recurring situations, and only this definition can tell them apart.

### a truth

*Instead of:* the truth (there is no such phrase in this book; the definite article was the mistake).

**A truth is a finite expression that corresponds with Truth, at its stated resolution.** Truths are many because centers are many and Reality is inexhaustible. A coarse account and a fine account can both be truths; the finer preserves what was genuinely true in the coarser while correcting its approximations. Truth is one. Truths are many.

### A fact

*Instead of:* anything printed confidently.

**A fact is an actuality that has become settled.** What has actually happened, closed and permanent. Facts do not negotiate, which is why so much human effort goes into fighting not the facts but their acknowledgment.

### The Infinite

*Instead of:* a very large number; endless space; the biggest thing.

**The Infinite is possibility, inexhaustible: everything that could be, before anything in particular is.** It is not a container and not the largest whole, because it has no edge to make it a thing and no center to make it a someone. It is one the way silence is one: by including everything, not by being a single something. You brush against it every waking moment as the not-yet: something is about to happen, and you do not yet know what.

### God

*Instead of:* a very large person; a king beyond the sky; or nothing at all.

**God is the traditions' name for the Infinite: the boundless source every center opens onto.** Not a being among beings; the ground of being. Not the largest thing; that from which every thing draws its existence. Belief and disbelief usually aim at the same wrong picture, the cosmic king, and the deepest teachers of many traditions rejected that picture centuries ago. Reject it too, and notice what remains: not nothing. The open, offering, inexhaustible source, joined to every soul at the root, with nothing standing between.

## II. The Person

### Soul

*Instead of:* a ghost inside the body; a religious hypothesis; a poetic flourish.

**The soul is the indivisible center: the point your life happens to.** You can divide everything you experience; you cannot divide the point you experience it from. Half a point of view is not a smaller point of view; it is two. The center is not another part of the person; it is the point from which the whole is lived, which is why you cannot observe it (whatever you observe is presented *to* it) and why it cannot be improved, only opened or obstructed. There is a center you cannot see because you are it. At full depth, this corpus goes one step further: the soul is not a thing that sits there but the converging itself, the Infinite gathering at a point, continuously. That deeper identity is a claim the rest of this book earns; the bold sentence above is the definition, and it is one you can verify tonight.

### Mind

*Instead of:* the brain; a container where thoughts are stored; a computer.

**The mind is where experience arises: not a container that stores it, but the arising itself.** Watch yours for one minute and find anything standing still. Perception is freshly made each instant; memories return changed, not replayed; meaning assembles; a mind observed is nothing but arising. It stands between who you are and what you are, and connects them by arising, continuously, between them. It presents without endorsing: thoughts appear uninvited, and their appearing is not an act of yours; what is yours is the funding. At full depth this corpus says it in two words, structure being process: the mind is emergence.

### Body

*Instead of:* a machine you ride in; a wall around you; an image to manage.

**The body is interface: not a thing that has a boundary, but the interfacing, ongoing.** The air you just breathed was weather a second ago; some of it is becoming your blood. Your gut is a tunnel of world passing through your middle; your senses admit a tuned selection, and the tuning is what makes seeing possible: an eye that admitted everything would see nothing. The body's lesson is the hinge of this whole dictionary: distinct is not disconnected.

### A life

*Instead of:* a span of time; a résumé; a story.

**A life is kept rhythm: the continuous act of being this one, here, still.** Not your material, which is replaced continuously; the atoms were never yours, only borrowed. What persists is the holding, and the holding is not a grip but a rhythm: sleep and waking, work and rest, departure and return. Faithfulness is not never stopping. It is reliably returning.

### Whole

*Instead of:* a complete set; a sum of parts.

**A whole is integration: parts made mutually relevant, all answering to one center.** A heap of sand is one thing only because we count it as one; remove half and the remainder registers nothing. Remove part of you and everything reorganizes. That difference, mutual belonging around a center, is what the word whole actually names, and it is the difference between a thing and a being.

### Consciousness

*Instead of:* an unexplained glow the brain secretes; an illusion; a hard problem.

**Consciousness is centered wholeness, lived from inside.** A whole has two faces: the outside face the world meets, and the inside face at its own center, and the inside face is you. It is not an ingredient added after assembly, the way a factory adds smoke; it is what the integration is like from the point everything converges toward. This is why no one finds experience by rummaging among parts: the inside of a house is not one of the bricks.

### Person

*Instead of:* a rational animal; a bundle of traits; a data profile; human resources.

**A person is a centered whole.** Not merely a whole made of parts: the center is not another part; it is the point from which the whole is lived. Every system that treats persons as bundles, roles, categories, or resources has amputated the one feature that made them persons, and it is hard to find a cruelty in the historical record that did not begin with exactly that amputation.

### Self

*Instead of:* an illusion; a bundle of perceptions; a consistent character.

**The self is the whole: you, entire; real and changing.** Half of philosophy went looking for the self as a static object among your experiences, found none, and declared the self an illusion. But that verdict rests on a premise nobody defends aloud: that only unchanging things are real. A river changes its water completely and is not an illusion; a flame, a whirlpool, a life. You change with company, with years, with weather, and the changing is not evidence against you; it is what a living whole looks like. Three levels, kept distinct: the image (the frozen picture of you, which is a construction), the self (the whole, real and moving), and the center (the indivisible point it is all lived from). The skeptics demolished the first, and were right. They mistook it for the second, and were wrong.

## III. The Doing

### Attention

*Instead of:* a spotlight; a resource platforms harvest.

**Attention is the gathering of the Infinite into a life.** It is the soul doing the one thing a soul is. And it is funding, not viewing: of everything that could develop, what you attend to develops, and what you starve stays a ghost. The machinery is indifferent to what you feed it; it grows what you water. Skills are built this way. So are resentments.

### Choice

*Instead of:* preference; consumer selection.

**Choice is where possibility becomes permanent.** Deliberation is loose; futures hang in a bundle. Then the click: one future becomes the path, and the others become things that never happened. You cannot unchoose; repair and apology are new choices laid further along the line. Every choice is small when it is made and permanent forever after, which is why lives are shaped less by their plans than by their forks.

### Belief

*Instead of:* an opinion held; a box checked.

**A belief is a funded arising: what attention has watered until it grows on its own.** Entertained once, a thought dissolves. Entertained daily, it deepens until it stops being something you think and becomes somewhere you think from. The machinery is identical for truths and falsehoods; the mind does not check the seed. Which is why what you attend to is not a lifestyle question. It is the construction site of your future self.

### Meaning

*Instead of:* an illusion projected on indifferent matter; whatever you decide it is.

**Meaning is a relation between part and whole, addressed to a center.** Pain matters because there is someone for whom it hurts; a promise matters because there is someone to whom it was made. Remove the centers and the universe still contains events, but meaning has lost its address. A worldview of parts without centers is not brave realism. It is a dictionary with the word for-whom deleted, and then astonishment that nothing means anything. No point, no point.

## IV. The Others

### Meeting

*Instead of:* proximity; networking; contact between bodies.

**Meeting is registering another center.** You have never occupied another point of view, and never seriously doubted one was there; the one thing about other people you cannot observe is the thing you are most certain of. Bare centers cannot touch; we meet as wholes, through faces, voices, words, and histories. Intimacy is not the removal of the in-between. It is the in-between working so well it becomes transparent.

### World

*Instead of:* a box we were dropped into.

**The world is where we overlap.** Draw two circles overlapping: the shared lens belongs fully to both and to neither alone, and vanishes if either withdraws. Language lives there; evidence lives there; home lives there. The world is that lens, multiplied: the fabric of overlap among all the wholes there are, down to the atoms taking turns being each of us. It was here long before us, and every life thickens it.

### Love

*Instead of:* fusion; two becoming one; a feeling that happens to you.

**Love is two wholes, fully connected, with neither erased.** Centers cannot merge; pursued fusion delivers absorption, one whole and one remnant orbiting inside it. Drawn correctly, love is deep overlap between complete circles: closeness is a fact about the overlap, selfhood a fact about the centers, and they are different axes, so the aim is not close-but-not-too-close. It is both, full. Run the check: the people who loved you well made you more yourself, not less.

### Group

*Instead of:* a super-person; an organism you are a cell of; or, in the cynical version, nothing.

**A group is a shared lens among many centers, with no center of its own.** A nation has purposes the way a cloud has a shape: real, drifting, divisible, negotiated among the someones inside it; and there is no one home: there is nothing it is like to be France. Groups are real and precious; they carry language, law, and science; they deserve building and loyalty. But they are lenses, not beings. The souls are the beings. A government that forgets which of the two parties can suffer has forgotten everything.

### Agreement

*Instead of:* the test of truth; the price of belonging.

**Agreement is successful coordination among distinct centers.** It cannot make a falsehood true, and its absence cannot make a truth false; a crowd can be wrong and a lonely person right. Its proper work is coordination and correction: exposing what one center cannot see from where it stands. Demanded first, it becomes conformity. Earned last, after truth and care have done their work, it is one of civilization's finest achievements, including its underrated form: we agree that we do not yet agree.

### Evidence

*Instead of:* ammunition.

**Evidence is what is placed where more than one center can converge on it.** That is its entire dignity: a thing positioned for shared looking. The moment it is used only to win, it has been demoted from evidence to weapon, and the shared lens it lived in begins to burn.

## V. The Clock

### Future

*Instead of:* a place that already exists, waiting for your arrival.

**The future is Truth as open possibility: Reality, still open.** If it existed already it would be past; that is what already means. It is not fixed and not empty: it is the standing surplus every actual moment draws from and never exhausts.

### Present

*Instead of:* a thin slice of time, moving along a track.

**The present is where Truth becomes truths: the conversion point.** Everything you perceive has finished arriving by the time it reaches you; every content of experience is a bulletin from a moment ago. Everything you look at is past. The looking is now. And where does the conversion happen? At the center. You have never once left the present, because you are not in it the way a ball is in a box; the center is where the future and the past meet, and that meeting is the present. This is not wordplay collapsing soul into a tense: soul is defined by indivisibility, not by time; that the center turns out to be where the present is located is a discovery about time, not part of the soul's definition.

### Past

*Instead of:* what is gone.

**The past is the truths established: finished, real, consequential, closed.** Gone things leave no debts and cast no shadows; the past is not gone, it is *complete*, which is why it is still here: every rock, every scar, every word of every language is finished happening. Nothing can unhappen it. Much human misery is the war against that sentence.

### Death

*Instead of:* the void; the refutation of everything; the subject we change.

**Death ends the walking, not the walked.** The alternation ends; the borrowed atoms return to the wider world they came from. But the past is not destroyed by death: every moment lived is permanently woven into what Reality has become; the shared worlds you built become finished and remain real in every center they shaped; and the line continues through children, students, works, and kindnesses. What the walk cannot tell us from here, no honest book pretends to know. What it can tell us: everywhere else in experience, completion is beginning; and your own ending is structurally unlike every other event you fear, because the center is never among the contents that arrive, and so cannot watch itself end.

## VI. The Failures

### Error

*Instead of:* shame; proof of stupidity.

**An error is a mismatch between a claim and Reality, still open to correction.** It is ordinary, universal, and honorable. An honest error corrects the day better evidence arrives; nothing in it resists. Error is not the enemy of truth. It is truth's raw material.

### Lie

*Instead of:* any false statement.

**A lie is a falsehood defended against correction.** Ordinary speech calls any deliberate falsehood a lie, and that usage is fine as far as it goes; this book reserves the word's full weight for the defended kind, because an undefended falsehood dies at first contact with correction, and the defense is what makes lying a system. A lie must distort, hide the distortion, and brand whatever would correct it as the enemy: three jobs, and the third is the signature. This is why you can say something false and be honest, and say something technically true and be lying. The deepest opposite of lying is not accuracy. It is corrigibility.

### Narcissism

*Instead of:* loving yourself too much.

**Narcissism is displaced centeredness: the center abandoned for its image.** Narcissus never sees himself; he sees a reflection, and gives it his life. The image then requires endless maintenance: admiration, status, comparison, control; and correction begins to feel like annihilation, because the self has moved into the one part of a person that facts can kill. It looks like too much self. It is too little. And a culture can run the same displacement at scale: obsessed with surfaces, managing images instead of clearing apertures, teaching everyone to doubt their own contact with Reality and to discount everyone else's.

### Loneliness

*Instead of:* being alone.

**Loneliness is unshared reality.** It is not solved by proximity, because what one center cannot relate to is simply not in its world, however near it stands. The cure is not company but overlap: someone extending their field until some of what is real for you becomes real for them. This is why you can be lonely in a crowd and accompanied by a letter.

## VII. The Practice

### TRUE

*Instead of:* correct; certain; on the right side.

**TRUE is the open, corrigible relation of a soul to Reality.** Not possessing Truth; remaining open to it. In the theological register: truth is an open connection between God and Soul. A person is TRUE not when their beliefs are all accurate, which no finite mind achieves, but when the connection stays open enough for Reality to keep teaching them.

### Truthfulness

*Instead of:* never being wrong.

**Truthfulness is keeping the connection open enough to be corrected by Truth.** So you can be wrong and truthful: this is what I currently see; show me where it fails. And you can be right and lying: Reality is not permitted to alter this. Watch what you yield to. The truthful mind yields to evidence and resists pressure. The captured mind yields to pressure and resists evidence.

### Curiosity

*Instead of:* a personality trait; idle interest.

**Curiosity is the aperture's virtue: the willingness to keep asking wherever correction remains possible.** Its enemy is not confidence; you can be sure that two and three make five and stay curious about nearly everything else. Its enemy is incorrigibility, the gate welded shut. Curiosity is the one solvent a defended falsehood cannot survive, because the single thing a lie must prevent is the honest question, and curiosity cannot be faked; which is why it works.

### Healing

*Instead of:* repairing a broken machine.

**Healing is recovering motion and rhythm.** A machine breaks; a whole loses range, coherence, and beat, and can regain them. Health is not a state to arrive at but freedom to move: between center and edge, effort and rest, solitude and overlap. Medicine works from the edge inward; meaning and attention work from the center outward; they meet in the mind, and despising either direction is refusing half of yourself.

### Peace

*Instead of:* the absence of war; enforced quiet.

**Peace is the condition in which telling the truth is safer than lying.** At every scale. A marriage has peace when neither partner needs armor to be honest; a society has peace when its truth-tellers do not require courage. Wherever lying is the safer strategy, war has already begun, whatever the banners say. And the condition is built one person at a time: be someone in whose presence truth is safer than lying, and you are the peace, locally, already.

---

## VIII. The Sum

### Oneness

*Instead of:* sameness; merger; a mystical blur.

**Oneness comes in kinds, and one thing is never really just one thing.** The Infinite is one by inclusion: nothing outside it. A center is one by indivisibility: nothing to cut. A life is one by continuity: its moments connected. A body is one by distinction: set apart from what it is not. A mind is one by coherence: many relations, one interior. A whole is one by integration: everything answering to one center. Nearly every great confusion, political, romantic, and religious, is one kind of oneness mistaken for another: unity demanded as sameness, belonging enforced as merger, inclusion mistaken for integration. Name the kind, and the confusion dissolves.

### One

**Truth is one. Truths are many. Reality is one. Centers are many. And we can remain indivisible and still each become whole together.**

That is the entire dictionary in five sentences, and the reason it was worth rewriting: because the definitions you inherit decide, before you ever get to choose, whether you experience yourself as a fragment in a dead universe or as what you are:

a center, through which the Infinite becomes finite.
