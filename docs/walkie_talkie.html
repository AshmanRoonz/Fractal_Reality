How it connects

Created: 2026-10-27
Last updated: 2026-10-28
Version: 1.4

Your voice goes straight from this device to theirs. The link is WebRTC, the browser's own peer-to-peer channel, encrypted end to end. No server carries the audio.

The only internet step is the introduction. To find each other, the two devices swap a few kilobytes of connection details, encrypted with the channel's key, through public Nostr relays. Once they are linked the relays are out of the loop. On the same Wi-Fi, or with one phone sharing a hotspot, the audio never leaves the local network: the LINK line on the radio shows the route it actually took.

Why not Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth? A web page cannot reach either one. No browser exposes Wi-Fi Direct, and Web Bluetooth can only connect out to gadgets like heart-rate straps (it cannot advertise), so two phones running a page cannot pair over it. A native app could, through Android's Nearby Connections or Apple's Multipeer Connectivity.

How devices remember each other. The first time you power on, this device makes its own key pair. When two devices meet on a channel, each proves its key to the other over the live encrypted link, so a remembered device is recognized by its key rather than its name. Next time, power on and the radio tunes your last channel and finds them by itself, with no codes.

Channels are a name and a PIN. Anyone who types both lands on the same channel, like a walkie-talkie's channel and privacy code, so pick a PIN that's hard to guess; a longer one is safer. A device you haven't met shows up as NEW, so you'd see a stranger arrive.

What is kept, and where. Only on this device: your key, your name, your channels and the devices you have met. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

When a direct link is blocked. Phones on different mobile networks, or on guest Wi-Fi that keeps devices apart, often can't reach each other directly. Then the radio falls back to a relay server (TURN, on Cloudflare) that passes the audio along still encrypted, so it can't listen in. The LINK line says RELAYED when that happens.

Diagnostics

If two radios won't link, copy this from each one and send it along: it shows how far they got. It holds no addresses or keys.
