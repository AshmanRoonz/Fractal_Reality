Three names for one energy at three positions: E when undifferentiated, 𝒫 when flowing through phase and time, m when closed behind a boundary. E is the future; it has not converged yet. 𝒫 = E / (i · t) is the present; it is the only one of the three with time in it, because power is the happening. m is the past; structure already committed. The center is where the future and the past meet, and that meeting is the present.

This page runs that sentence as a machine. A fixed pool of energy drifts as an undifferentiated shimmer: the future. Present points appear at an indeterminate rate and converge the flow through themselves. Each point that gathers enough walks the octave: it commits a line, the line branches, the branches close into a boundary, the interior fills as a field, and the whole completes. Completed wholes bond when they draw near; three bonded wholes can converge again into a greater whole that contains them. Wholeness nests; entanglement accumulates; the past grows more complex while the total never changes. A whole can also dissolve, returning its energy to the future pool, but its trace remains: you can dissolve a fold; you cannot un-fold it into exactly what it was.

What this is and is not. This is the ontology animated, not physics derived. The only physical law in it is bookkeeping: the unit total is genuinely conserved (every quantum is either free, in transit through a present point, or bound in a whole, and the three shares always sum to 1; the readout computes this live, it is not decorative). "Entanglement" here means persistent bonds formed by shared history, a visual analogy, not quantum entanglement. Spawn times are Poisson, so the number of present points is genuinely indeterminate. The triad rule (three bonded wholes may nest) is the observer-triad count T = 3 used as a design choice, not a demonstrated consequence. Old traces beyond a memory cap are culled; that is a limitation of the page, not a claim about the past.
