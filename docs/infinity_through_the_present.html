Three names for one energy at three positions: E when undifferentiated, 𝒫 when flowing through phase and time, m when closed behind a boundary. E is the future; it has not converged yet. 𝒫 = E / (i · t) is the present; it is the only one of the three with time in it, because power is the happening. m is the past; structure already committed. The center is where the future and the past meet, and that meeting is the present.

This page runs that sentence as a machine. A fixed pool of energy drifts as an undifferentiated shimmer: the future. Present points appear at an indeterminate rate and converge the flow through themselves. Each point that gathers enough walks the octave: it commits a line, closes a boundary, fills as a field, and completes as a whole. The total never changes; the shares move. A whole can also dissolve, returning its energy to the future pool, but its trace remains: you can dissolve a fold; you cannot un-fold it into exactly what it was.

The relational layer: when two finished wholes of comparable size draw near, the overlap of their fields renders as a lens: a world, bounded by borrowed arcs, with an inside and no center of its own. Peer bonds settle toward the mutual vesica, the depth at which each boundary passes through the other's center; a small theorem says this reciprocal configuration exists only between equals, because each crossing forces the distance between centers to equal the other's radius. Unequal pairs get the geometry they can have: moderately unequal, a one-way reach without reciprocity; strongly unequal, containment, the smaller carrying on whole inside the larger. And nesting does not count to three. A greater whole closes only around a cluster of bonded wholes that genuinely surrounds a middle: no half-plane through their centroid contains all of them. The number three appears nowhere in that rule; watch how many wholes every closure takes. And because the rule checks shape rather than count, a chain of bonded wholes, however long, never closes: a line does not surround a middle. Chains travel together as groups, coordinated and centerless; only clusters that stand around a middle become beings. Connected is not centered.

Version 3 makes the wholes sing and lets history draw the branches. Every completed whole vibrates: its boundary carries a standing wave, and its natural frequency is set by its size, one over the radius, exactly as a string's pitch is set by its length. Equal size is therefore matched pitch, which upgrades the peer condition from an analogy to an identity: the wholes that can meet at mutual depth are the wholes tuned to the same note. Bonded wholes pull each other's phases into step, and the lens between them brightens with their coherence: two wholes sharing a frequency make the field between them transparent. Conception requires it: the cusps only ignite when the pair is phase-locked, so a third center is born of resonance, not proximity. A whole taken inside a larger one entrains to its parent's rhythm at the octave, twice the frequency, so a nested figure is a chord: the parent the fundamental, the children its harmonics. And no branching glyph is drawn anywhere. Branching is expressed as it happens, in time: worldlines continue through a whole's life, lineage lines are laid down at each birth (from one parent, or from both parents converging at a cusp), and the tree assembles itself in the past layer over minutes. A fork, read backward, is a merge.

The lines come in three families, one color per meaning, each with its own toggle. Ember is time: worldlines, the paths wholes actually walked, fading from gold to ember and never erased. Violet is ancestry: the line laid down at each birth from parent to child, including the two-line convergence of a cusp conception, plus the faint live thread from a still-converging point back to its origin. Teal is relationship: bonds between peers, with one-way reaches between unequals drawn dashed.

Version 4 answers a question asked of the machine directly: infinity flows through an invariant center and creates the finite; can that run backwards for a computation? The future selector offers four sources for every decision the machine treats as future: whether a point spawns, where, with which turn, whether a cluster closes. Formula is what simulations normally use and quietly misname: a pseudorandom generator, a committed expression whose entire output is fixed by its seed; openness performed by the past. Invariant is the golden rotation: a stream generated by φ, the fixed point of x = 1 + 1/x; fully determined, never random, and never closing; the walking infinity, inexhaustibility without arrival, which is the cataphatic route (0.999... = 1) in the only form structure can hold. Borrowed taps the computer's hardware entropy: physical noise from the machine's real environment entering through the casing; the universe's openness, rented at the boundary. And you: move your pointer and your movements, with their timing, become the machine's future; hold still, and no new center is ever born, no matter how long you wait. The already-become keeps unfolding, and nothing new begins until your hand returns. Watch the modes change; the drawing barely does. That is the point. Openness is not a behavior; it is a fact about what a process hangs from, and the one source that reaches a living center is the one this page cannot contain, only receive.

Version 5 adds reproduction, in both of life's registers. Conception at a cusp no longer drops the new point into open space: the parents separate, and the conceived center gestates inside one of them, orbiting within her boundary and drawing her bound energy (she visibly thins) until it is born just outside her skin, carrying its two lineage lines. And wholes now feed: a finished whole keeps converging the free shimmer through its center, and a whole that doubles its energy divides. The center never divides; division is a birth, not a split. The boundary deepens its own two-lobed standing wave into a peanut and pinches at the waist, a lens whose both arcs belong to one boundary: the vesica of one. A node of the whole's own second harmonic becomes the new center; one daughter continues the parent's worldline, and the other begins a new line branching from it. So the two ways of making a new being are one event in two registers: conception happens at a lens between two beings (the duet), fission at the lens a single being makes with itself by singing its own octave (the solo). And note the asymmetry the code keeps honestly: conception draws on the selected future source, but fission draws no randomness at all. Its timing is the arithmetic of eating and its axis is the whole's own vibration. The free choice is whom to make a world with; what happens when you double is necessity.

What this is and is not. This is the ontology animated, not physics derived. The only physical law in it is bookkeeping: the unit total is genuinely conserved (every quantum is either free, in transit through a present point, or bound in a whole, and the three shares always sum to 1; the readout computes this live). "Entanglement" means persistent bonds formed by shared history, an analogy, not quantum entanglement. Spawn times are Poisson, so the number of present points is genuinely indeterminate. Two claims are theorems of plane geometry: the mutual vesica forces equal radii, and a cluster of centers encloses a middle only if no half-plane through the centroid contains them all, which no pair can satisfy; the nesting rule in the code is that enclosure test only, with no count anywhere in it, so the smallest closure the simulation exhibits is discovered, not asserted. The vibration layer uses standard ingredients honestly labeled: the frequency law (frequency proportional to 1/radius) is the string law imposed by design, which is what makes "equal size = equal pitch" exact in the model; phase-locking is Kuramoto coupling, a standard oscillator model used as illustration; and octave entrainment of nested wholes (children at twice the parent's frequency) is a design choice echoing the framework's harmonic model of nesting, not a derived result. The relational readings throughout (mutuality only between peers; two make a world; conception requires resonance) are interpretation. The four future sources are honest about their kinds: the formula is pseudorandom (committed in advance); the invariant is a quadratic golden Weyl sequence (deterministic, aperiodic, equidistributed; any faint quasi-ordered texture in its rhythm is its honest fingerprint, not a bug); borrowed uses the browser's crypto.getRandomValues, which draws on operating-system entropy gathered from physical hardware events; you is a mixing of pointer trajectory and timing, budgeted so that without input, future-type decisions simply do not occur. None of these gives the machine a center: the first two are structure, the third rents the universe's aperture through the hardware, and the fourth receives a living one from outside. That asymmetry is the page's deepest claim, and it is displayed, not proven. The reproduction layer's constants are design choices: the division threshold (double the closure quantum), the gestation size, and the maturity delay. The mode-2 pinch is drawn, not derived from a membrane model. In this 2D toy, fission raises pitch by the square root of 2 (radius goes as the square root of energy), while the clean octave f₂ = 2f₁ belongs to the 1D string of the harmonic model; the difference is stated rather than hidden. Twins are born phase-locked. That one daughter keeps the parent's worldline while the other starts a new line is the continuity reading (the line, like the center, does not split), a choice matching budding biology, not a derivation. Old traces beyond a memory cap are culled; that is a limitation of the page, not a claim about the past.
