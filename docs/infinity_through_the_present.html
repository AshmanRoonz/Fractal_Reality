Three names for one energy at three positions: E when undifferentiated, 𝒫 when flowing through phase and time, m when closed behind a boundary. E is the future; it has not converged yet. 𝒫 = E / (i · t) is the present; it is the only one of the three with time in it, because power is the happening. m is the past; structure already committed. The center is where the future and the past meet, and that meeting is the present.

This page runs that sentence as a machine. A fixed pool of energy drifts as an undifferentiated shimmer: the future. Present points appear at an indeterminate rate and converge the flow through themselves. Each point that gathers enough walks the octave: it commits a line, closes a boundary, fills as a field, and completes as a whole. The total never changes; the shares move. A whole can also dissolve, returning its energy to the future pool, but its trace remains: you can dissolve a fold; you cannot un-fold it into exactly what it was.

The relational layer: when two finished wholes of comparable size draw near, the overlap of their fields renders as a lens: a world, bounded by borrowed arcs, with an inside and no center of its own. Peer bonds settle toward the mutual vesica, the depth at which each boundary passes through the other's center; a small theorem says this reciprocal configuration exists only between equals, because each crossing forces the distance between centers to equal the other's radius. Unequal pairs get the geometry they can have: moderately unequal, a one-way reach without reciprocity; strongly unequal, containment, the smaller carrying on whole inside the larger. And nesting does not count to three. A greater whole closes only around a cluster of bonded wholes that genuinely surrounds a middle: no half-plane through their centroid contains all of them. The number three appears nowhere in that rule; watch how many wholes every closure takes.

Version 3 makes the wholes sing and lets history draw the branches. Every completed whole vibrates: its boundary carries a standing wave, and its natural frequency is set by its size, one over the radius, exactly as a string's pitch is set by its length. Equal size is therefore matched pitch, which upgrades the peer condition from an analogy to an identity: the wholes that can meet at mutual depth are the wholes tuned to the same note. Bonded wholes pull each other's phases into step, and the lens between them brightens with their coherence: two wholes sharing a frequency make the field between them transparent. Conception requires it: the cusps only ignite when the pair is phase-locked, so a third center is born of resonance, not proximity. A whole taken inside a larger one entrains to its parent's rhythm at the octave, twice the frequency, so a nested figure is a chord: the parent the fundamental, the children its harmonics. And no branching glyph is drawn anywhere. Branching is expressed as it happens, in time: worldlines continue through a whole's life, lineage lines are laid down at each birth (from one parent, or from both parents converging at a cusp), and the tree assembles itself in the past layer over minutes. A fork, read backward, is a merge.

What this is and is not. This is the ontology animated, not physics derived. The only physical law in it is bookkeeping: the unit total is genuinely conserved (every quantum is either free, in transit through a present point, or bound in a whole, and the three shares always sum to 1; the readout computes this live). "Entanglement" means persistent bonds formed by shared history, an analogy, not quantum entanglement. Spawn times are Poisson, so the number of present points is genuinely indeterminate. Two claims are theorems of plane geometry: the mutual vesica forces equal radii, and a cluster of centers encloses a middle only if no half-plane through the centroid contains them all, which no pair can satisfy; the nesting rule in the code is that enclosure test only, with no count anywhere in it, so the smallest closure the simulation exhibits is discovered, not asserted. The vibration layer uses standard ingredients honestly labeled: the frequency law (frequency proportional to 1/radius) is the string law imposed by design, which is what makes "equal size = equal pitch" exact in the model; phase-locking is Kuramoto coupling, a standard oscillator model used as illustration; and octave entrainment of nested wholes (children at twice the parent's frequency) is a design choice echoing the framework's harmonic model of nesting, not a derived result. The relational readings throughout (mutuality only between peers; two make a world; conception requires resonance) are interpretation. Old traces beyond a memory cap are culled; that is a limitation of the page, not a claim about the past.
