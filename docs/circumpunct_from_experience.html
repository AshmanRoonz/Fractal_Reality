The Circumpunct

A theory of wholeness, built up from direct experience.

The 0 is the inward unity of the 1.
The 1 is the integrated unity of the many.
And every 1 is part of a greater whole.

The circle that stays in view is a live model. It changes as you read, and some sections let you work it. Each section is marked with the kind of claim it makes, from direct experience to proposal.

1 · Direct experience

I am.

I exist. I experience. This is already so before any theory. I can doubt what I am seeing, and the doubting is still an experience.

So the walk starts here, with no equipment. Everything later has to be reachable from this spot, or has to say plainly where it stops being reachable.

StandingDirect experience. Nothing below can add to it or check it.

2 · Direct experience

I am one and many.

Attend for a moment and the one experience turns out to be full. There is a body, with organs and cells. There is sensation, thought, memory and feeling. Hunger and a plan for tomorrow can be present at once. They are distinct, and they belong to one life.

So the first structure is already here. I am a whole with parts. I also live among others and within a world, so I am a part as well. Wholes have parts, and wholes are parts of greater wholes.

StandingDirect experience, then a first generalization.

3 · A simple law

Many is not yet one.

A heap of sand can be counted as one heap. I differ from a heap because my parts bear on each other. Each one takes in what the others are doing and answers.

Here is about the simplest law that does this. Each part fades if left alone, reads the others, and has a limit to how strong it can get.

x′ = −x + tanh(G · what x reads)

3.0

Below a gain of 1 the parts fade. Above it they pull each other into a shared state that nothing outside supplied, and it holds. That is a first kind of whole. Its middle is an average, the way any list has one. Cut it in half and each half has an average of its own and carries on.

StandingA law chosen for its simplicity. The threshold at a gain of 1 is arithmetic, and the cut was tested in a model.

4 · Arithmetic

The 0 and the 1.

Now go round the parts in order, and let each differ a little from the one before, so that the circle of them turns once. Two things are then true of the same parts. Add up their states and they cancel to 0. Add up their steps and they make exactly 1 turn.

The 0 is the center of a 1, but not just any 1. It is the center of a 1 that is itself a whole with parts.

4

The inner figure lays the states end to end, and it closes. Two parts can balance on a 0 and cannot turn, since two leave no way round. If every part is alike, nothing turns and nothing cancels. That is a 1 with no center. Four parts a quarter turn apart are 1, i, −1 and −i, the framework's four strokes, and they add to 0.

StandingArithmetic.

5 · Theorem and model

What cannot be divided.

Only 0 is unchanged by dividing. Half of 0 is 0, and each half is all of it.

Give each part a phase as well as a strength, and close the loop. Mathematics then supplies something nobody put in. If a loop turns once and stays alive all the way round, a continuous field inside it must have a zero. Nobody places it. It is none of the parts, and there is no half of it. The whole and its center also arrive together, because the loop's turn and the zero's charge are one fact read two ways.

Try it. Press on the field, and that part is held opposite for as long as you press. Then cut the whole.

In the modelHolding any one part opposite turned at most .5% of the others, and removing the parts nearest the center changed nothing, in every run. So no part is the center, and no part can take it over. At a gain of 4, a cut left the turn with the piece that kept the zero: 1 and 0. A cut through the zero left 0 and 0. A turn is counted in whole numbers, so there is no fraction to be had. A plain list and a star with a hub, each started with a turn, lost it in every run.

For a conscious being the framework goes further. There is an indivisible center of experience, a point of view from which the many are experienced as one. This is what it calls the soul. An observer is ultimate to itself, at least.

The model does not reach that. A spiral in a chemical dish has a zero like this one. So the model shows a shape a center can have, and it leaves open whether anything experiences from such a center.

StandingArithmetic, then a theorem, then a model. The soul is a proposal about the first person, and no test here touches it.

6 · Definitions

Reading the symbol.

The sign ⊙ can now be read as a working description of a whole.

○
Boundary. The body as interface, where I am distinct and where the world touches me.
Φ

⊙
Whole. All of these integrated: ⊙ = Φ(•, —, ○).

These are roles, and they need not be places in space. They can describe a cell, a person or a community. The boundary distinguishes without severing. At the center we are absolutely distinct. At the boundary we are distinguishable but permeable. In the field we overlap. In Reality we were never separate.

7 · Model and reading

The present.

The past is what has emerged: structure, memory, consequence. The future is what has not yet emerged: possibility. The present is where they meet, at the center.

The future converges. The past emerges.

In the model, let each part turn at a rate equal to its own energy, since the framework treats energy and frequency as one thing. At the zero there is no energy, so nothing turns there. With nothing else tuned, lines of equal phase run in toward the 0 and end there, and signal runs outward from it. Nothing crosses the 0.

In the modelWherever a single 0 held, in all 16 cases mapped, phase lines ran inward in every run. Signal ran outward in all 6 cases run. Give the center 25% more gain than its field and the flow stops. Give it more and the flow runs faintly the other way. Give it less and it carries about three times the slope and five times the signal. The prediction had been the reverse. Too strong a turn breaks one 0 into many, from 16 up to 178 of them.

AgainstA whole fed only through its center stayed about the size of that center, reaching 6 to 10 sites out. When its input stopped it went out, and when the input came back it returned with no 0. The wholes that reached far were powered by their own parts. That sits against a plain reading of energy converging through the aperture, and it is unresolved.

The framework says the aperture has one variable, how open it is, and that the practice is to clear the aperture and tune the field. In this model, feeding the center obstructs the flow. An input with no phase of its own also cannot land on a 0, since gain times 0 is 0, so it can act only through the parts around it. Beside those two results stands the principle: the finite directs how the infinite converges and emerges. I use what I have become to participate in what I am becoming.

8 · Model and adjudication

Whole and part, without end.

Parts have their own centers. It is circumpuncts all the way down. There is always another whole, and in the mereology of wholes and parts there is no top whole.

How can a whole of wholes hold together? A part's states add to 0, so nothing can read a part by averaging it. What a part gives its whole is its rotation, and the whole can reach that only at a contact.

In the modelPlaced in one shared field, centers turning alike pushed each other out: 13 fell to 5 or 3. Joined at one contact each, every level kept its own 1 in every run, through 64 parts and three levels. A whole made of such parts held with 5 of them, where a plain ring at the same gain needed 9. Cutting the whole's ties left every part its turn. A part with no turn could sit in a whole with one, so the runs show this nesting can be so. They do not show it must be.

Groups are wholes too, and there are many types of center. These are the ones the tests have told apart.

9 · Proposal

The whole without a boundary.

Follow the question to its limits. God is an unbounded whole, ∞, the inclusion of existence itself. We can call God the greatest whole, and God is still not the top rung of the chain. Going from one whole to the next is a step along the chain. Going to ∞ is a step out of it.

Souls are the indivisible centers of experience within it. In this theology neither arose from an original division. The proposal goes as far as this: God needs us just as much as we need God, since a whole and its parts are mutually necessary to what that whole is.

In the modelThe runs bear on one side of that. A whole on a ring lost its turn when it lost a part, while parts kept their own turns when the whole's ties were cut. There the parts need the whole in order to be its parts, and the dependence for going on existing runs one way.

StandingA metaphysical proposal. It is offered as one, and nothing here derives it.

10 · Practice

Attention.

Back to myself. Everything asks for me at once: pain, hunger, a memory, tomorrow. I do not experience all of it equally.

Attention is where the will of the whole meets the demands of its parts.

Will directs attention. Imagination configures the mind. The body responds. The whole changes state. As I walk I can imagine a more coherent way of moving, and my posture, breathing and balance can change, with no command to any one muscle. If I imagine danger my heart may race. My mind is a field that runs through my body, and influence goes both ways.

1

In the modelSuppose everything that passes through one point shares one limit, as one energy suggests. Then the more the point carries, the dimmer each thing and the less it takes to change any one. That limit is an assumption, and these numbers rest on it. The likeness to attention is a reading. Separately, a change that has taken hold in this law stays when the push is removed. Changing state is not the same as reversal, and going back would be a second change.

ElsewhereImagined exercise raised heart rate and breathing in step with the amount imagined (Decety and colleagues, 1991), and later work limits how far that reaches. Alexander technique lessons helped chronic back pain out to a year in a trial of 579 patients. Neither establishes the mechanism proposed here. If pain does not lessen, nobody has failed, and new or unexplained pain still needs examining.

Self-mastery is the growing ability of the whole to coordinate its parts without becoming captive to any one of them.

11 · Model and reading

Without domination.

If every person is a whole with their own interior, then no person is only a tool, a role or a diagnosis. Every person is both whole and part, and a healthy relationship has to respect both. The framework holds that health is a corner and not a middle: full participation and full integrity together.

A healthy whole protects the integrity of its parts while remaining capable of correcting itself.

ElsewhereIn existing survey data the two coordinates do come apart in people. Fusion and cutoff, read here as the two ways of leaving that corner, correlated only .29 in 351 adults, and well-being was highest where both were low. That tests the shape and none of the laws.

When a greater whole demands that its parts surrender their integrity, it undermines its own wholeness. When a part refuses every responsibility to the whole, it undermines what sustains it. A center that ignored its parts still held together in every run, so holding together is no sign of health. What that center loses is the ability to change when its parts do.
