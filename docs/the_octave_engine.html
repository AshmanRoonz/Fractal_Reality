The earlier pages in this series showed energy passing through a finished whole. This one shows the whole being built, station by station, in the constitutive order the framework walks: the octave.

Energy converges toward a point, and the point ignites: that is • fed by ⊛, the aperture and its convergence. What emerges from the center is not a spray but a line, the committed wake of the convergence: —. The line does not become an edge by itself; it branches, and the branches curve around the center, wrapping it: ⎇, the i-turn, where extension becomes enclosure-in-progress. When the wrapping arms meet each other on the far side, the boundary closes: ○, and there is suddenly an inside where before there was only a middle. Then the field builds within the boundary: ✹ into Φ, arriving energy no longer spent on structure but held, circulating, filling the enclosed interior. And when the interior is full, the octave closes the way the framework says it must: the completed whole becomes the aperture of the next scale, 3.5D = 0D′, and a new line grows outward from the finished boundary toward a larger one. Three octaves nest, the whole figure breathes for a moment, and then everything relaxes back into the shimmer it was made from, and the cycle begins again. Nothing is created or destroyed at any point; the readout proves it continuously.

What this is and is not. The ontology animated in its constitutive order, not physics derived. The one law is bookkeeping: a fixed count of quanta, each of which is at every moment free (shimmer), in flight (converging), committed (a structure segment: every segment of line, branch, and boundary is exactly one quantum, frozen), or held (field), and the total is conserved and computed live. The radii of the three octaves, the costs of the stages, the branching rate, and the field capacities are design constants. The walk itself is the framework's corrected ladder, drawn: convergence makes the point and leaves the line as its wake; line branches into boundary; boundary emerges into field; field recurses back to the point at the next scale. The dissolution at the end of each cycle is entropy in the corpus's sense (constraints relaxing, the 1 tending back toward itself) and is what makes the cycle honest: without it the engine would consume its own substrate and stop.
