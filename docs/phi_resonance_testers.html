Help test the Android app

Created: 2026-10-18 · Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Version: 1.2

Phi Resonance, the app version of the φ-Entrainment sound lab on this site, is on its way to Google Play. Before a new app can go public, Google asks for at least 12 people to test it for 14 days in a row. If you have an Android phone, you can be one of them. Joining takes about five minutes.

What you need

  • An Android phone with the Google Play Store.
  • The Google account (Gmail) you use on that phone.
  • A way to pay on Google Play: Google has testers of a paid app buy it, the same as anyone else.

iPhones can't join a Google Play test. An iPhone version comes later.

How to join

  1. Join the testers group

    Open the group while you're signed in to the Google account from your phone, and tap Join group. That puts you on the list of testers, and it's where you'll hear when the test opens.

    Join the testers group

  2. Become a tester

    When the test opens, you'll hear about it in the group. Then open the tester link with the same Google account and tap Become a tester. Until the test opens, the link doesn't work yet.

    Open the tester link

  3. Install it from Google Play

    The tester page then links to the app in the Play Store. It's a paid app, so Google Play asks you to buy it; then it installs like any other app. If it doesn't show up at first, give it a few minutes and look again.

  4. Keep it for 14 days

    Please stay in the test and keep the app installed for at least 14 days in a row; Google counts the days. Open it now and then: start a session, try a few presets, and use headphones for the binaural ones.

Tell us what you think

Anything helps: what you liked, what confused you, what didn't work, what you wish it did. Post it in the testers group, which works like a small forum: you can read what other testers found and reply to them. A sentence or two is plenty.

Post in the testers group

Good to know

  • The app collects nothing about you: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your settings stay on your phone. (Privacy policy)
  • Joining the group shares your email address with the developer, only to run this test.
  • Start at a low volume. The app can also show flashing light, off unless you turn it on; skip it if flashing light affects you.
  • It is a sound tool for relaxation, focus and exploration. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

Curious before the test opens? Try the web version in your browser.
