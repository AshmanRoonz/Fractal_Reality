⊙ must state its price for every bit before that bit arrives (a Krichevsky-Trofimov estimator), so a context seen once cannot fake certainty; fresh contexts cost a full coin-flip. Shrink the ring and give ⊙ time: the substrate closes its loop first, ⊙ discovers it later, and the lag between the two floors is how long the center lived inside a cycle it couldn’t feel. Widen the ring beyond ⊙’s memory and observation budget and the floor stays out of reach: the entropy lasts exactly that long.
