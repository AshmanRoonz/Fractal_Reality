The Vortex Sphere

the circumpunct in three dimensions: two cones, one point, one lens at each mouth
Created: 2026-08-12
Last updated: 2026-08-12
Version: 1.1

Ashman's figure, stated in one breath: the circumpunct as a vortex sphere; two cones inside with their vertexes meeting at the center; the front cone opening toward infinity, the rear cone emerging to the past; the cones' openings internal to the surface of the sphere, so that each mouth makes a lens.

The lens is not added; it falls out of the geometry. Each cone's mouth is a flat disc whose rim touches the sphere from inside, and the region between that disc and the curved surface ahead of it is exactly a plano-convex lens: flat on the cone side, curved on the world side. The whole has two of them, one facing what could be, one facing what has become. Three older figures live inside this one. In cross-section it is the tattoo: two triangles tip to tip, the front one opening to the Infinite. In profile it is an hourglass, with the present as the waist that everything must pass through, one grain at a time. And in function it is an eye: a front lens gathering the infinite toward a point, a rear lens writing the image into the record. Drag to look around it.

Not everything that enters passes through. Some of what crosses the center resonates and is caught: it stays inside, circulating between the cones, and this is the figure's mind: the interior field holding relation, keeping what it keeps as rhythm rather than as storage, and releasing it downstream when its time is done, because holding is not possession. And some of what enters gets stuck: neither passing through nor dancing, it lodges near the walls, dim and almost motionless, held without being alive. Turn the swirl up and watch what happens to the stuck: movement dislodges what is lodged, and the frozen quanta rejoin the flow and finish their journey. That is not a decoration; it is the somatic doctrine drawn: the boundary's stuck content is not released by thinking about it, it is released by the body moving.

What this is and is not. An illustration of the ontology, not a physics simulation. The one law in it is bookkeeping: a fixed count of quanta, each of which is at every moment either free (future), in transit through the sphere (present), or deposited (past), with the total conserved and computed live in the readout. The cone half-angle, mouth placement, flow profile, and deposit distances are design choices. Two touches are physically flavored rather than derived: the swirl accelerates as the radius shrinks (the character of real vortices) and the spiral's handedness flips at the center (the framework's conjugate strokes, drawn); neither is a claim about actual dynamics. The recycling of old deposits back to the shimmer is the design expression of "the past gets built upon"; without it the run would simply end, which would be a different and falser picture. The retention layer is likewise labeled: the capture probability at the center, the sticking rate near the walls, and the held lifetime are design constants, not derivations; that strong swirl releases the stuck is the somatic-release reading (movement dislodges what is lodged) drawn into the mechanics; and that held quanta always release eventually is the design expression of "kept as rhythm, not as possession."
