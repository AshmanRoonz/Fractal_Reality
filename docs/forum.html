What happens inside

The forum is a practice space for becoming more conscious together: exploring new perspectives, building the strongest version of each other’s positions before answering them, and treating a changed mind as a win rather than a loss.

Ideas : share what you are seeing; a thought, a connection, a draft of something true.
The Steelman Arena : bring a disagreement and build the strongest version of the other side first.
Corrigibility Practice : say what changed your mind, and what corrected you.
New Perspectives : try on a view you do not hold and report what the world looks like from inside it.
Open Questions : what exists? What matters? Ask what you actually want to know.
