Listening safely

Headphones for binaural and φ-cascade modes (stereo separation matters). Drone, harmonic, chord, monaural and noise modes play fine on any speaker. Start at a low volume and raise it slowly; if anything becomes uncomfortable, lower the beat rate, the reverb and the volume. This is a sound tool for relaxation and exploration, not a medical treatment.

Before you begin

  • Turn your device volume down first. Sessions fade in over a few seconds; raise the level slowly to a comfortable, quiet volume.
  • Use headphones for binaural and φ-cascade modes, at a low level. Long, loud listening can harm hearing.
  • Do not listen while driving, operating machinery, or doing anything that needs your full attention.
  • If you have epilepsy or a history of seizures, avoid fast pulses and the visual flash, and talk to your doctor first.
  • This is a sound tool for relaxation and exploration, not a medical treatment. Stop if you feel unwell.
