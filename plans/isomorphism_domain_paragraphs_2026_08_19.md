## The design: six slots, so the set performs the claim

Every paragraph below has exactly six sentences, in a fixed order:

1. **The whole**: the domain's own circumpunct, anatomy named in domain words (a center that gathers, something that holds, a boundary that filters, an interior that relates).
2. **The becoming**: the four beats as the domain does them.
3. **The wrap**: completion opening as the next beginning.
4. **The nesting**: wholes within wholes along the domain's own chain.
5. **The two failures**: the domain's Inflation and Severance, in its own vocabulary.
6. **The fingerprint**: one established, public fact of the domain that is the architecture showing, stated at honest strength.

The set performs the isomorphism instead of asserting it: read a row and you get one domain, whole; read a column (the same slot across all ten) and you get one invariant in ten dresses. Same skeleton, different substance, which is the claim itself in typographic form. Register rules: plain words, no notation, no framework integers; a domain's own public numbers are allowed where they ARE the fingerprint; no em dashes anywhere.

## The ten paragraphs (drafts)

**Matter**

> An atom is a whole: a nucleus that gathers, an identity that persists through time, an outer shell that decides what touches it, and an interior cloud where everything relates. Matter forms the same way everywhere: energy converges toward a point, holds, closes a surface, and fills an inside. Every finished whole becomes a building block: particles close into atoms, and the closed atom is the unit the next structure begins from. Quarks sit inside nucleons, nucleons inside nuclei, atoms inside molecules, planets inside systems, galaxies inside the universe, and physics has found no floor below and no ceiling above. Matter fails in two directions: bound so hard the parts lose all identity, or so loosely that nothing holds, and everything that lasts sits between. The fingerprint is wherever physics looks: any bounded field with a center rings with its own natural notes, in a drumhead, a star, an atom, and the hum of the sky itself, the same law wearing different numbers.

**Molecules**

> A molecule is atoms made mutually relevant: gathered into shared centers of charge, held by bonds, wearing one electron surface that decides what can react, keeping one interior arrangement. Chemistry is the same walk at the bench: attraction converges, a bond commits, a stable shape closes, and a shared cloud fills the inside. Every finished molecule is a part in waiting: amino acids complete, and proteins begin from them. Atoms nest in molecules, molecules in polymers, polymers in the machinery of cells, and the chain climbs without a break. Reactions fail the two ways: fused until the parts lose their character, or inert so that nothing joins, and every working chemistry lives between. The shapes repeat because the constraints do: the same few bond angles and the same shell counts appear in every laboratory and every star, on the far side of the universe as surely as here.

**Life**

> A cell is the textbook whole: a nucleus that gathers, a lineage that holds (every cell from a cell), a membrane that filters, and a cytoplasm where everything relates. Life builds by the same walk: molecules converge, a lineage commits, a membrane closes, and an inside comes alive. Every completed whole opens the next: a finished cell is the beginning of a body, not the end of a story. Organelles nest in cells, cells in organs, organs in bodies, bodies in the living world, wholes made of wholes the entire way. The body knows both failures by name: the cell that stops answering to the body and grows as if it were the whole, and the tissue whose parts stop holding together at all. The code of life is the architecture made visible: four letters read three at a time, the same spelling in every living thing ever examined.

**Mind**

> A mind is the whole you know from inside: an attention that gathers, a memory that holds, a filter that decides what gets in, and an inner field where everything that arrives relates. Every thought becomes part of you by the same walk: entertained, repeated, habituated, fused; attention converges, repetition commits, habit closes, and identity fills. Each completed understanding becomes the center of the next: what you master becomes the place you see from. Thoughts nest in habits, habits in character, and each mind inside a family of minds; the inner life is nested exactly like the outer one. Minds fail in the two directions: a self swollen until nothing outside it may correct it, and a self denied until nobody is home to be corrected; health is the free range between. The fingerprint is now textbook: perception runs on prediction, the mind meeting each moment with boxes built from its past.

**Persons together**

> Between two people the same anatomy appears: each a center, each with a history that holds, each with boundaries that filter, and between them a shared field where the relationship actually lives. Every real bond walks the beats: attention converges, commitment extends, the boundaries become honest, and a shared interior fills that belongs to neither alone. Every completed bond becomes a center of something larger: two people close into a family, and the family begins. Persons nest in families, families in communities, and each level is fully whole while fully a part. Love fails in exactly two directions: absorption, where one person disappears into the other, and abandonment, where the connection is cut; the whole of the matter is what those two exclude: connected without dissolving. Every durable moral tradition converges on the same instruction from its own direction: keep your center, honor the other's, hold the connection; the convergence itself is evidence that the shape is real.

**Society**

> A society is a whole of wholes: persons as its centers, institutions that hold across generations, membership that filters, and a common life where everything relates. Every community forms by the same walk: people converge on a shared purpose, commit it into institutions, draw a boundary of belonging, and a public interior fills. Every completed community becomes a member of a larger one: households into towns, towns into nations, nations into a world still learning to hold together. The nesting runs the whole ladder: person, family, community, nation, humanity, each level real, none of them final. Societies fail in the two directions and in both at once: the collective that erases its members, the crowd that shares nothing, and the modern ache of belonging to nothing while being no one. Cities keep the signature in numbers: double a city and its pipes and roads grow less than double while its ideas grow more, the same economy of scale that governs living bodies.

**Spirit**

> Every tradition draws the same anatomy in its own names: a source nothing stands outside of, a spark at the center of each person, a faith that holds, a practice that filters, and an inner life where the source is met. The spiritual walk is the same walk: turning toward, staying faithful, becoming distinct, being filled. Every completed soul opens outward: the finished inner work becomes a beginning for others, which is why the saints end up surrounded. Soul nests in community, community in the divine, and the traditions agree about the top of the ladder: it is not a bigger rung but the ocean the ladder stands in. The scriptures name the two failures in every language: claiming to be the source, and denying there is any source at all. Traditions that never met keep converging on this same structure, a oneness beyond names and a spark that is somehow the whole of it, and the convergence of the maps is the oldest evidence for the territory.

**Mathematics**

> The circle is the simplest whole mathematics owns: a center, a reach that holds, a boundary drawn, an interior enclosed. A proof walks the same beats: a definition converges, a lemma commits, the cases close, and the theorem's interior fills with consequences. Every proved theorem becomes a starting point: yesterday's conclusion is today's tool, endings that are exactly beginnings. Numbers nest in sets, sets in structures, structures in theories, and mathematics proved its own ladder has no top: no final system can complete itself. The discipline knows its two failures: a formalism mistaken for the whole of truth, and intuition with no rigor to hold it; working mathematics lives between. One constant appears wherever anything closes around a center: measure any circle in any universe and the ratio of edge to width is the same; closure has a cost, and the cost never changes.

**Music**

> A melody is a whole made of wholes: each note complete, all of them held on one line, bounded by a beginning and an end, alive with harmony inside. Music is the walk made audible: a tone converges out of noise, rhythm commits it, phrasing closes it, and harmony fills it. The octave is the wrap you can sing: climb the scale and the final note is the first note of the next octave, an ending that is exactly a beginning. Notes nest in phrases, phrases in melodies, melodies in movements, and a single string sounds all its harmonics at once, wholes within wholes inside one tone. Music knows the two failures as sounds: the scale with no home note, which drifts and cannot end, and notes with no relation, which are only noise; music is what lives between. Every musical culture found the octave on its own: halve a string and it sings the same note higher; in music the architecture is not a theory but one of the oldest measurements in the world.

**Healing**

> Every healer meets the patient as a whole: a person at the center, a history that holds, a body's defenses at the boundary, an inner life within; treat only the part and the treatment misses. Recovery walks the beats: attention converges on what is wrong, commitment to care holds, protection closes around the wound, and life refills the inside. Every recovery becomes a foundation: the healed place is where the next strength starts. Healing works at every level at once because the levels are nested, and the two roads meet in the middle: medicine enters from the boundary inward, meaning works from the center outward. Illness takes the two shapes, and so does care: the part that grows as if it were the whole and the parts that stop answering each other; the cure that erases the person and the neglect that abandons them. The oldest clinical fact is the architecture at work: bodies mend themselves when the conditions are held, so the healer tends the boundary while the whole does the knitting.

## Protections, pinned

**(a) Fingerprint honesty.** Slot 6 uses only established public facts of each domain, or an explicitly external witness (Society's urban scaling is flagged as cited literature, swappable by decision 5). No framework-specific numeric claims appear anywhere in the set; the only numbers are the domains' own public ones (four letters, three at a time).

**(b) The failure slots type to the Lies without collision.** Each slot 5 is the domain's Inflation and Severance; Society's carries the both-at-once third state (anomie), consistent with the standing pin (two ways, two axes, three unhealthy corners), and it belongs in the sociology paragraph because that is the word's home discipline.

**(c) Scope of the soft paragraphs.** Spirit describes what traditions converge on; it is not confessional. Healing describes structure, not treatment; nothing in it is medical advice. Persons together states the corner claim, not a counseling method.

**(d) The parallelism is mechanical, not decorative.** Six sentences per paragraph, fixed slot order, verified by count; if editing breaks the slot structure, the set loses the property that IS the demonstration, so edits should preserve slots even while rewording freely.
