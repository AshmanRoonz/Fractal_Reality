## The angle, and what makes it a different book

Three books now share the material, and each has a different door:

- **What Is Wholeness?** walks the octave: how a whole forms. Its subject is column 3's interior (the four kinds of 1). Ontology-first.
- **One** is written in the singular: the forgotten center, the reader's own seat, narcissism as the seat surrendered, the healing as coming home. Its subject is column 2, first person. Diagnosis-first.
- **This book** is written in the plural, and the plural is the load-bearing novelty. "OUR points of view": many seats, one Reality, many results, and the question that only exists in the plural: **can the results be shared?** Its subject is the whole table at once, and especially what happens BETWEEN columns 2: agreement, science, love, consensus, and their failures (the strawman society, the war on the settled). Relationship-first.

The one-sentence theme, upgraded by the plural: **Reality, flowing through our points of view, becomes the world we share.** The singular table's sentence made a world; the plural table's question is whether it is the same world. Canon already holds the deep answer (section 27.7l.1): every center computes pi from its own seat and every center gets the same pi; **consensus reality is the fact that pi is pi from every point of view.** Shareability is not a social accident; it is a property of Reality being one while the seats are many.

The subtitle is the engine question: How the Infinite Becomes Finite. This is the mini book's crown sentence ("We are each a center through which the Infinite becomes finite") promoted to a book-length answer, and it is the E to P to m cascade in plain words: the book can carry the physics lightly because the physics IS the table.

## Candidate structure (four parts; three columns and the meeting)

**Part I: Reality.** The Infinite; the future; the ocean before any label. One by inclusion. Why there is always more (inexhaustibility); why Reality never argues, only answers (error as its only voice); why the future is open. God as classical theism holds God: not a being among beings. The reader's relationship to column 1: curiosity, the open connection.

**Part II: Our Points of View.** The seats; the present; one by indivisibility. Why MANY (the Soul Array: A1 forces multiplicity; there was never going to be just one aperture). Perspective as access, not possession: partial is not false. The equidistance dignity: no privileged seat; every center is the 1 at its position (made in the image). Hume's search and why it fails. The width of now is zero. The begotten, not the built.

**Part III: The Result.** The settled; the past; wholeness as four kinds of 1 that are 1. Mind, body, world all on the settled side (even your mind is a result; perception lags). The fossil principle (the disc is the memory of the water that made it). The dignity of the past: what became, became; i squared equals minus one; even Reality does not take it back. Grief and consolation live here.

**Part IV: The Meeting.** The plural's own part; the book's reason to exist. Two seats, one Reality: agreement as the composed whole (the fifth pillar; two-party wholeness). Science as the discipline of sharing results (the explicit formula of culture: what any seat can check from its own chair). Love as perfected mediation (resonance; the field at transparency). The failures, now as column confusions between people: the strawman society (results arguing with results, seats vacated); DARVO (defending a result against Reality by attacking another seat); the gradient law (rooms set the price of truth) and the repair (run different rooms). The healing, plural: bidirectional corrigibility (my image of myself may be wrong; my image of you may be wrong); the encounter as the thing the whole book was for. Closing: the world we share is the result we keep making together; keep your souls corrigible, and let reality ripple those ponds.
