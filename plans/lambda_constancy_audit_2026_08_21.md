## The commission, and what the audit actually found

The commission assumed the question was whether the corpus's constancy commitment is forced or leaves room. The audit found something better and stranger: **the corpus is two-voiced on w, and the second voice already predicts evolving dark energy, publicly, with a stated kill band, from before the current data existed.** The adjudication is therefore not "commit or hedge"; it is which of two dated strata is canon, and what each future does to each.

## Voice 1: constancy (the ladder stratum)

- **§12.2 / §27.7g:** Λ = α^(SU(3)·R) · (1 − 6α + 4α²) / 72, a structural constant computed from α and pool integers, landing on the observed value at 0.004%. A constant, not a function of epoch.
- **The Ladder Correction Notice (2026-06-09):** one of the two stated gains of the corrected ladder is that "Φ at 3D matches dark energy's constant energy-per-volume character." Constancy is used as a physics witness FOR the corrected ladder.
- **CLAUDE.md §10.10a:** dark energy is "Φ itself ... volumetric, matching Λ's constant energy per unit volume."

## The data state (verified by web search, 2026-08-21)

DESI DR2 (March 2025) reports a 3.1σ preference for evolving dark energy over ΛCDM from BAO+CMB, rising to about 4.2σ with supernovae; the central w₀w_aCDM fits sit near (w₀, w_a) ≈ (−0.75, −0.86): a LARGE evolution, phantom in the past, crossing −1 at low redshift. Euclid DR1 is expected October 2026; DESI DR3 follows.

**Comparison, stated carefully:** §12.1's form shares the direction (w rising with time from below) and the existence claim (a deviation DESI can see) with the current data trend, and §12.1 named DESI and a sigma level in advance. But its amplitude is roughly fifty times smaller than DESI's central fit, and it never crosses −1. At current survey precision the §12.1 point is nearly indistinguishable from ΛCDM itself, so DESI's present preference for evolution is a preference away from BOTH corpus voices, not a vindication of either. Honesty requires both halves: the direction call is looking prescient; the amplitude is not currently supported.

## The futures table (the audit's core deliverable; verdicts pre-assigned before the data)

1. **Constancy confirmed (w → −1.000 ± 0.005):** the evolution voice dies by its own stated band (predictions page and essay item 3 retracted with visible notices); the ladder voice stands; the corrected-ladder witness stands.
2. **Small deviation confirmed (near w₀ ≈ −1.02, |w_a| ≲ 0.05, no crossing):** the evolution voice is spectacularly vindicated as a dated, specific, pre-DR2 functional form; the ladder voice's constant-Λ formula is reframed as the base value the small evolution rides on; the "constant energy-per-volume" witness for the corrected ladder is weakened and the notice takes a caveat; the §12.1 mechanism is promoted for re-derivation under current canon (its fitted parameter must be closed or the vindication is partial).
3. **Large evolution confirmed (DESI-DR2-like central, w₀ well above −1 with crossing):** BOTH voices die at face value: constancy by the data, the §12.1 form by amplitude. The framework takes the loss; any new derivation produced after confirmation is graded post-hoc, per the pre-registration protocol's spirit.
4. **Ambiguity persists (2σ to 4σ, dataset-dependent):** both voices stand as rivals; this table remains in force; nothing is promoted or retracted.
