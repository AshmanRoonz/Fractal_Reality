## The paragraph (draft, for countersign or edit)

> One architecture appears everywhere because there is only one thing becoming, and what we call domains are not separate worlds: they are the same becoming met through different boundaries. So when an atom, a cell, a person, and a planet show the same shape, that is not resemblance and not metaphor: whatever closes around a center is forced into the one architecture, because closure has no alternative. Strip away what a thing is made of, its sizes and speeds and materials, and what remains is the skeleton of wholeness: a center that gathers, a line that holds, a boundary that filters, an interior that relates, and the closed loop running through them; whatever shares this skeleton is the same in everything the skeleton forces and different only in dress; change the substance and the numbers change, never the architecture. Beneath the anatomy is the event that deposits it: possibility, met at a point of view, becoming a settled world; every domain tells all three under its own names. The claim's power is that whatever the shared shape forces can be proven once and spent everywhere: a law first heard in a vibrating string is found again in the shells of the nucleus, the folding of a protein, the shape of a life, and the keeping of a promise. It is why the same shape keeps being found by people who never met, in cavities and cells, in scriptures and equations, and why the same small counts keep turning up in the code of life and the constants of matter: often enough to bet on, never enough to stop testing. The claim polices itself: an analogy breaks when you push it, and this must not break; wherever the shape is truly absent the claim dies there, and resemblance alone earns nothing until the forcing is checked. Even the understanding that found all this obeys it: as it was refined, the dressing changed and the skeleton survived; what recurs across the world recurs again in the story of coming to see it.

Eight sentences, 340 words, no notation, no framework integers; the only count in it is the event's three parts.

## Finding 3: protections, pinned

**(a) "Domains are views" is not anti-realism.** The clause says domains are views OF one process, not that they are illusions: the lens limits light, and that is how it forms an image; a view is a real encounter at a real boundary. Pinned against a deflationary reading.

**(b) "Beneath" means deposited-by, not prior-to.** The anatomy is the event's deposit (v2.12), so the event is derivationally prior and the anatomy is what it leaves; "beneath" locates depth of explanation, not temporal order. Same axis discipline as the standing pins.

**(c) The honesty rider is load-bearing.** Deleting "often enough to bet on, never enough to stop testing" in a later edit would silently change the claim's epistemic strength from calibrated to triumphal; it is this paragraph's "should."

**(d) The transfer sentence is conditioned twice.** Only what the shared shape FORCES transfers (not everything true of a string), and only where the skeleton is verified (the typing discipline). Both conditions are in the wording ("whatever the shared shape forces"; "until the forcing is checked").
