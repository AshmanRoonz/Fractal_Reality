## 1. The proposal, compactly

The opening guard: energy and information are not numerically identical; there is no universal E = I, only a conversion relation (Landauer: E_dissipated ≥ k_B·T·ln2 per bit erased; the cost per bit depends on temperature and implementation).

The compact quadruple:

> **Energy is potential distinction. Information is realized distinction. Structure is retained distinction. Dynamics is distinction flowing.**

The station dictionary: ∞ = undifferentiated possibility space; convergence = conditioning, selection, compression, measurement; • = a realized outcome from a point of view; — = channel and causal history; ○ = the partition defining distinguishable states; Φ = the probability distribution and relations among states; ⊙ = an integrated information-processing system; ⟳ = output becoming the next scale's input or prior.

The boundary principle: without a partition, H = log₂1 = 0 regardless of how rich the possibility space is; with a boundary distinguishing N equiprobable outcomes, H = log₂N. **Information does not exist merely because possibilities exist; information appears when a boundary makes possibilities distinguishable.** The center receives the realized outcome with surprisal i(x) = −log₂p(x).

Information balance: with C_I converging and E_I emerging information rates and stored M, Ṁ = C_I − E_I; ◐_I = C_I/(C_I + E_I); bounded memory with continuing flow forces the throughput-weighted long-run ◐_I → 1/2; stability requires (M − M*)(C_I − E_I) < 0: information homeostasis.

Closing formulation (staged canonical-wording candidate): energy is the physical capacity for transformation; information is transformation made distinguishable relative to a boundary; matter is information retained as stable structure; a whole is a bounded system that acquires, stores, transforms and releases physically instantiated information.

## 3. Precisions required before canon (staged for adjudication)

1. **Whose boundary is the partition?** The information cycle as proposed (possibilities → partition → selection → ...) walks boundary-before-convergence, which is not the constitutive order (• → — → ○ → Φ). The corpus already owns the resolution machinery: two channels. Measurement is the validation direction, and the partition π belongs to the OBSERVING whole's ○ (its filter), while selection realizes the observed •. Under that reading the cycle is the ⊛ channel of an observer, not the genesis of a whole, and the order tension dissolves; but this is an adjudication, not a footnote, because it decides which whole each dictionary row describes.
2. **Information is not conserved, so the accounting needs its full form.** Copying and computation create correlations (internal generation G_I) and erasure destroys them (internal loss L_I, the channel Landauer prices). The balance statement should be carried as Ṁ = C_I − E_I + G_I − L_I with the constitutive principle applying to totals, exactly per the protocol's §13 accounting guard; the two-channel form in §1 is the special case with G_I = L_I = 0. Without this repair the layer would pathologize every computing system.
3. **The Φ name collision.** "Integrated information-processing system" for ⊙ invokes IIT, whose central quantity is also called Φ. The corpus's Φ is the field; IIT's Φ is a scalar measure of integration. A disambiguation note is mandatory wherever the layer is written up.
4. **Landauer's bound is an inequality with stated scope** (classical bits, erasure, temperature T); the bridge should always be quoted as ≥, never as an equation, and the layer's opening guard (no universal E = I) should travel with every restatement.
