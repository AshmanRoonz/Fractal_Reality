## Finding 2: four arguments

1. **One way (Ashman's).** A dimension has two directions; a 1D line is traversable either way. Becoming runs one way. The point plus one direction is a ray (a half-line), and the ray completed is the line: the record of becoming, which can be read in both directions and rewritten in neither (§4.11a: "prediction reads, never rewrites"). The ten-stations table already glosses 1D as "timeline." Canon already holds the asymmetry the argument needs (§4.11): "Space is the lateral relationship between 0s at the same scale (symmetric; peers). Time is the vertical relationship across scales (asymmetric; fold direction). i² = −1 prevents true time reversal."
2. **The kinematics.** §4.11a (countersigned, grade A): "The future converges. The past emerges." The future arrives at the center directly from ∞: the 2026-08-01 sharpening says the radial order read outward "opens directly onto ∞ through no channel at all, which is why the aperture's only variable is openness." A station on the way out of the point is a channel, so convergence cannot be one. What leaves the point is the past being made, which is emergence.
3. **The pump order.** Φ(t+Δt) = ✹ ∘ i ∘ ⊛[Φ(t)], read in canon as convergence ("gathering inward toward center"), then i ("90° transformation at the gate"), then emergence ("radiating outward from center"). §27.7r's own sentence puts the conversion at the aperture: i "converts convergent energy (⊛) into emergent energy (✹) at the aperture." The ladder begins at the gate (0D •), so in the pump's own order the first motion away from the gate is emergence. The old gloss put convergence on the far side of the gate, reversing the pump.
4. **The physics distinction.** In relativity the time coordinate is a full dimension: an axis with both directions, and the fundamental dynamical laws are, to excellent approximation, invariant under time reversal (the small T-violation of the weak interaction aside). Causation is not symmetric: an event can influence only its future light cone, one nappe of the double cone, and no signal runs backward. "Time is half a dimension" is therefore a claim about time as becoming, the one-way reach, and not about the coordinate; physics already separates the two, so the claim costs nothing against relativity. Pin against the §4.11a light-cone pin: that pin assigns the tenses to the two doors (becoming at the aperture; records arriving at the boundary); this claim counts directions, and traffic through either door runs one way. Candidate consequence, not adopted: the 0.5D constant c is the slope of the future cone's edge, the reach of the outbound stroke, so "the speed limit of convergent propagation" would read as the speed limit of the outbound stroke; the c formula's arithmetic is untouched.
