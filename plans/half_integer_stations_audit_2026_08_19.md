And ∞ is labeled ∞D in the ten-stations table, not "dimension minus one": the corpus already refuses to put the source on the ladder.

## Finding 2: the two registers of convergence, and what the 0.5D coordinate actually is

The proposal reads 0.5D as a source-and-target diagram (convergence sitting "between" point and line, taking the point as input). The coordinate is not that. The half-integer axis is a **phase address**: i¹ at 0.5D, i² at 1.5D, i³ at 2.5D, i⁰ at 3.5D; four quarter-turns, i⁴ = 1 at the tonic. "The first quarter past the tonic" locates a stroke on the cycle the way a clock face locates a motion; it does not claim the motion's fuel comes from twelve o'clock. And the corpus holds two registers of convergence that the proposal merges: the **genesis convergence** (A1 and D1; the ∞ → • move; carried by ▸, no coordinate), and the **pump convergence** (⊛, the perpetual inward stroke of a living whole, whose intake is always the local ∞ and whose deposit is structure). The proposal's relocation demand is satisfied by canon's first register; its deletion demand targets canon's second, which is a different object.

## Finding 3: the proposal's missing piece is the wrap, and the tonic already answers it

The sharpest diagnosis available: the proposal flattens the octave to a line (∞ at the top, ⊙ at the bottom, one operator per gap). On a line there is no slot for a process that ARRIVES at the point, so convergence gets pulled up to fill the ∞ → 0D gap, and a new operator must be invented for 0D → 1D (continuation, which is A2). On the helix, the arriving process already exists: **⟳ at 3.5D ≡ 0D′ is the process that enters the point, and it shares the point's residue class**: the double-natured tonic of the wrap lemma's own decomposition, R = T + T + 1 = 2T + 1, where • and ⟳ are one class. Entry and departure are both at the tonic's neighborhood: ⟳ arrives (i⁰), ⊛ departs (i¹). The question "does convergence come before the point or after it" is malformed at the beat level (∘ means paired, two views of one constraint, explicitly not sequential) and well-formed at the wrap level, where the answer is: what enters the point is the completion of the octave below. The proposal collapsed the arriving stroke and the departing stroke into one operator and then correctly noticed the collapsed thing fits no single coordinate. It does not; the two strokes fit two.

The half-step lattice is not notation riding on the theory; it is the coordinate system the quantitative layer runs on. Deleting the coordinates while keeping the operators forfeits, at minimum:

5. **The one place the number is also a geometry, where it checks.** D = 1 + ◐ = 1.5 at balance is measured: Brownian paths, coastlines, DNA at 1.51 ± 0.02, HRV. The burden statement, plainly: any replacement architecture must re-derive R = 7, the mod-3.5 wrap, the half-argument exponents, and the 69/31 split, or accept losing them. "Cleaner" that costs the predictions is not cleaner; it is the framework with its arithmetic removed.
