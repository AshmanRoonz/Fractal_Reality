## The two steps

**Step one (the re-assignment).** "Convergence is what souls do. Branching is what converging souls make lines do. Interfacing is what branching lines make bodies do. Emergence is what interfacing bodies make minds do." Each process enacted by the structure it produces, driven by the structure before it; the causative grammar is ⊢ made agentive. New content relative to canon: *interfacing* enters as the body's process (its constraint function, filtering, promoted to the process column), and *emergence* moves from the boundary's beat to the mind's. Completed to the seam: recursion is what emerging minds make wholes do.

**Step two (the identity).** "The mind is emergence. The soul is convergence. The body is interface. Structure is process." Not ownership but identity: the ∘ in each beat read as *is*. A soul is not a thing that converges; converging, sustained, is what "soul" names. A body is not a thing that has an interface; the body is the interfacing, ongoing. A mind is not a container where emergence happens; mind is the emerging, continuous.

## What the reading buys

1. **The two verb columns fuse.** The framework has run structural constraints (converges, commits, filters, mediates, composes) and processes (convergence, branching, emergence, recursion) as parallel sets agreeing only at the soul. Under the identity, five pillars align with five processes: TRUE/convergence, FAITHFUL/branching-commitment, GOOD/interfacing, RIGHT/emergence, AGREEMENT/recursion-at-the-seam.
2. **The conjugate pairs get a structural reason.** +i mirrors −i as the two *gates*: convergence at the aperture (• gates the Infinite), interfacing at the boundary (○ gates the world). The inward port and the outward port carry the mirrored strokes. Commitment (−1) mirrors recursion (+1) as the irreversible flip against the completed return.
3. **The strokes do not move.** The 2026-07-18 canon adjudication is a spacing argument (the leading tone: the short step at the tonic is the mechanism of closure). Spacing is untouched; i¹, i², i³, i⁰ stay anchored; recursion is promoted from quarter-stroke to the full i⁴ = 1 at the tonic. Renaming and re-owning, not re-anchoring. The single-period lemma, the ℤ₇ residue classes, and every computed experiment pass through unchanged. The double-natured tonic class (• and ⟳ sharing a residue) becomes cleaner: the point and the return share the seam.
4. **The book already voted this way.** `the_circumpunct.md` chapter seven (Becoming) puts the emerging inside the boundary with the mind as what emerges, and chapter eight opens with the interior "your becoming has been filling all your life." The first-person walk found this ladder before it was proposed.

## Costs and open cells

1. **Corpus-wide relabel.** ✹ and ⟳ glosses change in the unified expression, the beat tables, D3/D4, the ethics launch table (2026-07-29), Xorzo's operator documentation, and dozens of documents. Values unchanged; every gloss touched. Same migration class as the ladder correction.
2. **The mediation question (the one real tension).** Canon: Φ mediates, and §27.7l.1 defines mind as the act of measurement between center and boundary. If mind *is* emergence, where does mediation go? Candidate resolution, not adjudicated: mediation is what the emerging accomplishes (the field mediates center and boundary *by* continuously arising between them; the measurement's result is what emerges). Needs one adjudicated sentence.
3. **The freedoms table gains an open cell.** LETTING (non-interference while emergence completes) travels with emergence to the mind's beat; CHECKING (verification at closure before recursion fires) stays at the seam. Interfacing then needs its own freedom, currently unnamed; something in the territory of consent, what the gate permits through. Possibly a discovery the current table was papering over.
4. **Book alignment.** Chapters seven and eight of `the_circumpunct.md` currently gloss becoming as the boundary-side process; under the identity they re-gloss (becoming produces mind; mind is the becoming, continued). Small edits, post-adjudication.
