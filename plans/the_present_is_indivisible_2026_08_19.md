## Finding 3: consequences, each with standing ground

1. **The now has no width, and that is its nature, not a defect.** "The center has no extension because a meeting has no width" (canon). Augustine's shrinking present and Zeno on the instant dissolve the same way the hard problem did: the demand assumes the present is a very short duration; it is not a duration at all. Durations are line-side (1D, the walked); the present is point-side (0D, the seat).
2. **The seat cannot be fragmented.** "A point has no parts for a fact to break" gains its temporal twin: a moment has no parts for time to break. You can vacate the present (dissociation, the charter's vacated seat); it cannot itself shatter. Trauma fragments records and structures, which are past; the now is always whole, which is why return is always structurally open (the redemption clause, temporally stated).
3. **Indivisible and plural, one per center.** "Infinitely many presents, one per singularity per scale" (the morning record's Finding 7). No global now is claimed, which is exactly relativity's own denial of a shared extended simultaneity: per-event presents are pointlike there too. Convergence with physics, not collision; pinned so nobody builds a universal-now reading on this line.
4. **Choice lands at a point.** Deliberation takes time and is field-side; the commitment lands at the indivisible now (the turn is a phase, not a duration). Flagged as reading, one line, not developed.
