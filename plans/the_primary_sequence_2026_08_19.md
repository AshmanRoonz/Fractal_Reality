2. **The genesis sequence.** ∞ → •∞ → ⊙∞: infinity, convergence into the points, completion emerging as the foam. The four words are the genesis read per instance.
3. **The countersigned kinematics.** "The future converges. The past emerges." with the silent center between them: the four words are the kinematics with the two nouns restored (∞ is the future; the point is the present; what emerges is the past).
4. **The master table read as a sequence.** Reality (column 1), converging (Moves-by row), your point of view (column 2), emerging (Moves-by row), the result (column 3). The four words are the flow triad with its two motions spoken; the third column is emergence's deposit and needs no fourth word.
5. **The glyph itself.** ⊙ is the four words drawn: the circle converging to the dot; the dot emerging to the circle. The framework's own symbol has always been the primary sequence in one mark.

## Finding 2: the adjudication it delivers: convergence goes before the point, cleanly

The standing tension (canon speaking both launch-language, "• launches ⊛," and production-language, "convergence makes the point," reconciled only by the non-sequential ∘) resolves in the production direction at the primary layer: **the primary sequence is coordinate-free, and in it convergence precedes the point, because convergence is what makes the point** (D1's own content: "self-limitation requires convergence; localization is a process, not an instant"). The framework paragraph already speaks this order ("it limits itself... through infinitely many dimensionless centers"; "convergence makes a point"), as does the countersigned kinematics. The definition "convergence = 0.5D" is retired as a definition; what remains at 0.5 is an address (Finding 4).

## Finding 3: where the ladder goes: nothing deleted, everything re-seated

The octave is the primary cycle at high resolution; the primary sequence is the octave at four words. ## Finding 4: the two-layer rule (the glyph-integer template applied)

The same split lands here: **the meaning layer is the primary sequence (coordinate-free: infinity, convergence, point, emergence, with the deposit's anatomy inside emergence); the arithmetic layer is the half-step lattice (the stroke addresses 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, which the staged balance record reads as d + ◐ at the balance fixed point).** The addresses keep the entire formula layer unchanged (R = 7 as residues, the mod-3.5 wrap, E(1.5), E(2.5), A(3.5), the sum-21 route, the ℂ⁸ split, the engines); the primary sequence keeps the ontology honest (no process is defined as a fractional spatial dimension; the number is bookkeeping, and per the balance record it is not even arbitrary bookkeeping: it is ◐, theorem-forced).
