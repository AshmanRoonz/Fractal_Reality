## Finding 1: the four words have five independent canon homes

1. **The pump cycle, verbatim vocabulary.** The original three-stage temporal process is "⊛ (Convergence): gathering inward toward center; i (Aperture rotation): the turn at the gate; ✹ (Emergence): radiating outward from center," running on E = the one substrate. The four words are the pump with its substrate named and its turn implicit at the point.
2. **The genesis sequence.** ∞ → •∞ → ⊙∞: infinity, convergence into the points, completion emerging as the foam. The four words are the genesis read per instance.
3. **The countersigned kinematics.** "The future converges. The past emerges." with the silent center between them: the four words are the kinematics with the two nouns restored (∞ is the future; the point is the present; what emerges is the past).
4. **The master table read as a sequence.** Reality (column 1), converging (Moves-by row), your point of view (column 2), emerging (Moves-by row), the result (column 3). The four words are the flow triad with its two motions spoken; the third column is emergence's deposit and needs no fourth word.
5. **The glyph itself.** ⊙ is the four words drawn: the circle converging to the dot; the dot emerging to the circle. The framework's own symbol has always been the primary sequence in one mark.

## Finding 2: the adjudication it delivers: convergence goes before the point, cleanly

The standing tension (canon speaking both launch-language, "• launches ⊛," and production-language, "convergence makes the point," reconciled only by the non-sequential ∘) resolves in the production direction at the primary layer: **the primary sequence is coordinate-free, and in it convergence precedes the point, because convergence is what makes the point** (D1's own content: "self-limitation requires convergence; localization is a process, not an instant"). The definition "convergence = 0.5D" is retired as a definition; what remains at 0.5 is an address (Finding 4).

The octave is the primary cycle at high resolution; the primary sequence is the octave at four words. 1. **It is the master table's Form row with the Moves-by row between its columns.** Form: Infinity / Singularity / Wholeness (adjudicated); Moves by: converging / the turn / emerging (countersigned v2.16). The primary sequence is those two rows read together. And the provenance is already on file: the June 27, 2025 post's triad (Singularity, Wholeness, Oneness) is recorded in the charter as the anchor document, thirteen months early; the sequence closes a loop the corpus opened before its notation existed.
2. **It is the three forms of oneness joined by the two motions.** §4.8a, adjudicated: ∞ one by inclusion, • one by indivisibility, ⊙ one by integration. The sequence walks the three forms in order with converging and emerging between them: the entire ontology as three kinds of one and two motions.

Convergence is the ∞D-to-0D passage through that meeting; emergence is extension unfolding 1, 2, 3 in the corrected order (line, then boundary, then field).

### Finding 7: one precision pin (against the hollow-shell misread)
