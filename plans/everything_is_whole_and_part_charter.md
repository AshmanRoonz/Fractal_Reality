## The book

*Everything Is Whole and Part: Body, Mind, Soul, and Our Place in Reality*, by Ashman Roonz. A philosophical book grounded in personal experience, for an interested general reader who does not know the Circumpunct Framework. Plain language; no equations; testimony, argument, and empirical findings kept distinguishable; no invented memoir. Its opening statement:

> I am whole. One whole body. One whole mind. One whole soul.
> I am part. One part of Earth. One part of a greater whole. One part of Reality.

What it adds to the corpus is its angle: the framework met from inside a life (a part that feels like the whole, whole-body attention, structure to flow within, the courage corrigibility takes), with every framework claim offered as a proposal answerable to evidence. Its register is deliberately more cautious than the corpus, and several of its commitments pull against corpus positions; those are the rulings below.

## Readiness verdict (2026-10-04)

There was enough in the repository to draft every chapter, and not enough to finish the book. Four parallel research passes mapped the Development Map against the repo, and the key citations were spot-checked. Three kinds of material are missing, and the repo cannot supply them:

The 2026-10-03/04 discussion behind the interlude (the VR mesh, the boat, the black-hole image, "infinitely finite" in its current sense) is not in the repo: framework work in the repository stops on 2026-08-21.
2. **The two essays Chapter 2 cites,** "One, Many, and the Connection Between Them" and "The Argument from One and Many." They are not in the repository or its git history under any name.

## Rulings the first draft assumed

Each entry: the question, the default the draft took, why, and what overturning it would change. In the manuscript, the default is marked **[RULING R#]** where it bites.

**R1. Is Reality a whole?** *Default:* Reality is one by inclusion, not a centered whole; it is not part of anything and has no point of view of its own. The title then holds of every thing within Reality, taken one at a time, and "part" keeps its meaning. *Why:* this is Ashman's adjudication of 2026-08-01 ("there is no greatest whole; every whole is also part, while infinity is one without being whole"; CLAUDE.md, Wholeness Plane notice, item 4), and it answers the manuscript's own "reach of the title" note in the way that note asks. One's master table agrees (Reality's form is Infinity; Wholeness belongs to the result). *Bites:* Chapter 9 (argued), Chapters 1, 7, 8, 12 (consistent usage). *Overturning:* the book would need a different answer to "what greater whole could Reality be part of?" and a formal revision of the 2026-08-01 ruling.

**R2. Broad and strict "whole."** *Default:* "whole" keeps Chapter 1's broad sense (anything identifiable as one); "centered whole" is the strict sense (many parts integrated around one center), and the account of consciousness as centered wholeness applies only to centered wholes. The six kinds of oneness keep the senses apart. Whether Earth, a society, or a relationship is a centered whole stays open, with the groups record's test (one indivisible center, versus a center-shaped region occupied by several) offered as a test, not a verdict. *Why:* the manuscript uses "whole" broadly (a cup, a word, a group of apples) while canon reserves it for integration, and the countersigned scope "all wholes, cost named" (`plans/countersign_batch_2026_08_19.md` item 2) would make every broad whole an experiencer. *Bites:* Chapters 2, 3, 7, 8, 12. *Overturning:* adopting canon's narrow "whole" means revising Chapter 1's definitions; adopting the broad sense without the strict one makes the consciousness proposal cover cups.

**R3. Where is the aperture?** *Default:* at the center; its only variable is how open it is. What the interlude's boundary sentence describes (how much I take in, how quickly I respond, when I need space) is the body's gate. Two openings: the aperture faces Reality and truth; the gate faces the world. *Why:* the interlude places the aperture both "in that boundary" and "around the center"; canon places it at the center (CLAUDE.md: "its only variable is how open it is... clear the aperture, tune the field"), and *The Circumpunct* already names the pair ("You have two gates," `the_circumpunct_book.md` l.254 and l.888). *Bites:* the interlude (flagged below), Chapters 4, 10, 11. *Overturning:* putting the aperture in the boundary contradicts the interlude's own corrigibility section and canon's "a sealed center."

**R4. Does ethics follow from the structure?** *Default:* the values are stated as premises (experience, agency, and welfare matter), and claims about interdependence combine with them; the framework's stronger claim, that its ethics tracks the conditions under which wholes stay whole, appears only as a further proposal. *Why:* the manuscript's notes require it ("Being part of Reality does not, by itself, establish a duty"), while canon derives ethics from structure (§25.7b: "Ethics is NOT imposed on physics; it's the SAME structure"; the framework paragraph: "the whole of ethics is the both-and they exclude"). *Bites:* Chapters 7, 11. *Overturning:* the book would assert derivation, and Chapter 11's premises section would be rewritten as a proof.

**R5. The defended image.** *Default:* defensiveness is judged by behavior; the framework's proposal (the seat of viewing relocates into an image, so correction feels like an attack on the self) appears as a pattern anyone can fall into, explicitly not a clinical account; the center is denied, not destroyed. *Why:* the manuscript's commitment ("A person's point of view remains present even when their response is defensive or dishonest") against One's charter ("the center was vacated first"; "nobody home, the seat vacant," l.25, l.43), which also claims clinical agreement that the repo's own thesis qualifies (`protecting_the_lie_thesis.md` l.122). *Bites:* Chapter 11, the interlude. *Overturning:* the book would carry One's hotter register.

**R6. Energy and power.** *Default:* the physics meanings are stated (energy measured in joules; power the rate of energy transfer, in watts), and the framework's triple (energy is what could happen; power is what is happening; mass is what has happened) is presented as a philosophical reading of time's structure. *Why:* the manuscript asks for the separation; the framework says the power equation is "not an analogy... it IS the same equation" (`circumpunct_framework.md` l.2564). *Bites:* Chapter 10.

**R7. Dimensions are roles.** *Default:* point, path, boundary, and field are roles in the anatomy of a whole, with the manuscript's geometric caveats. *Why:* the manuscript's notes; canon reads the integer stations literally (body as a two-dimensional surface, mind as a three-dimensional volume), which collides with the manuscript's body of organs. *Bites:* Chapters 3, 9.

**R8. Mind as field, arising.** *Default:* "field" (the manuscript's word) for what mind is like; "arising" for how it comes to be, once the body's closure gives a life an inside. *Why:* book canon since 2026-08-08 is mind as emergence (`plans/structure_is_process_2026_08_08.md`); the manuscript says field. Both are kept. *Bites:* Chapter 3.

**R9. Continuity belongs to the anatomy.** *Default:* the opening statement stays body, mind, soul; the anatomy chapters add continuity, the thread of a life through time. *Why:* the foundations file and the plain guide count the line as part of consciousness; the manuscript's commitments fix the opening statement. *Bites:* Chapter 3.

**R10. "Infinitely finite" is a hypothesis.** *Default:* composition everywhere and without a final layer is presented with its motivating reasons, its counter-considerations, and what would challenge it; it concerns extended physical reality, so the indivisible point of view is not a counterexample. *Why:* the manuscript's notes; no argument in the repo meets their standard (the vacuum essay runs on the retracted ladder; self-similarity's axiom status is open decision 2). *Bites:* Chapters 9, 12.

**R11. Recursion keeps internal organization.** *Default:* a completed whole can act as one participant at a larger scale while keeping its organization intact. *Why:* the manuscript's notes; canon agrees ("whole in itself, small from above," `docs/the_holon_evolved.html` l.68). *Bites:* Chapters 3, 9.

**R12. The fifth question is named integrity.** *Default:* the manuscript's five questions (truth, faithfulness, good, right action, integrity) are kept in its order, with agreement presented as integrity between people. *Why:* the framework names the fifth pillar AGREEMENT; the manuscript's order is otherwise the adjudicated formation order. *Bites:* Chapter 11.

### Two foundation sentences flagged (left unchanged in the manuscript)

1. **Chapter 1, "Everything and Every Thing."** "*Everything* directs our attention to Reality considered as an all-inclusive whole," and "Reality, when defined as all-inclusive, needs to be treated as that limiting case." Under R1 the first reads correctly only in the broad sense, and the second suggests Reality is the top of the stack, which canon denies (the step from the greatest finite whole to the infinite is categorical, not vertical; the limit is "approached and never occupied"). A possible revision: "*Everything* directs our attention to Reality considered as all-inclusive: one because nothing lies outside it," and "Reality, when defined as all-inclusive, is not the top of that stack. It is a different kind of one: the one in which every whole is, rather than one more whole above the others."
2. **Interlude, "A Boundary That Supports Movement."** "The aperture is the adjustable opening in that boundary." Under R3, a possible revision: "That boundary has an adjustable opening, a gate. It concerns how I engage, how much I take in, how quickly I respond, and when I need space... At the center there is a second opening, which I call the aperture: how open I remain to what is true, including what I would rather not hear." This keeps "the aperture as adjustable openness" from the manuscript's commitments and matches the interlude's own later line, "openness around the center can narrow."

## Corpus issues found during the review

These belong to the corpus, not the book; they are recorded here so a future session can adjudicate them.

1. **The baroreflex claim.** §27.7r (`circumpunct_framework.md` l.22168) says that at about six breaths per minute "breath and heart rate are in quadrature." The resonance-breathing literature reports heart rate and breathing in phase at resonance, with heart rate and blood pressure 180 degrees apart (Shaffer and Meehan 2020; Vaschillo; Lehrer). The "most directly measurable i-stroke in a human body" rides on this claim. It needs checking and a correction note.
2. **`docs/why_space_can_never_be_empty.html`** is built on the retracted ladder ("field (Φ, 2D)"), carries no correction notice, and calls its argument "proof that the mediating surfaces exist at every depth."
3. ***What Is Wholeness?*** l.1565 ("The relationship becomes another whole.") and l.1651 ("The living Earth is whole.") predate the 2026-08-08 groups record.
4. **"Greater whole" is split.** The groups record of 2026-08-08 ("There is always more Reality. There is not always another whole.") stands against *Theme and Variations* ("A society is a whole of wholes"; "every whole is a part of a greater whole") and the 2026-08-21 synthesis. One ruling should settle it.
5. **Split-brain.** One's draft and charter (l.55) say "one and a half has never been proposed"; Nagel (1971) argued the number of minds may have no whole-number answer.
6. **Clinical claim.** One's charter (l.22) says "The clinic agrees: the grandiosity sits over an empty core," while `protecting_the_lie_thesis.md` (l.122) notes the "mask model's mixed empirical record."
7. **Mediated or direct.** CLAUDE.md "Topology of Connection" (l.496: "always mediated") was not updated for the 2026-08-01 notice and the countersigned mission statement (the aperture opens directly onto the infinite, "unblocked, not delivered"; person-to-person connection stays mediated).
8. **The present at the boundary.** `circumpunct_from_the_beginning_plain_v0_1.md` l.141 ("The present becomes concrete at the boundary") survives the 2026-08-01 sharpening (the present is at the center).
9. **"Infinitely finite"** lives only in `docs/ethics.html`, which has no header block or revision history.
10. **Two files label real people** and should not be quarried: `docs/narcissists_are_our_awakening.html` (a named social-media account) and `Path_of_Learning/narcissism_study/narcpatterns.txt` (a critic's GitHub posts filed under the narcissism study). Both conflict with this book's standard (judge behavior, not labels) and with the protecting-the-lie preprint's own rule that it "does not authorize diagnosing individuals at a distance."
